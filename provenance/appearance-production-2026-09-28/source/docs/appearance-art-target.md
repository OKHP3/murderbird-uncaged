## Ranked acceptance checks

1. **Restore the face and crown as one recognizable profile.** Keep the deep convex bill root and decisive downward hook, with a distinct open cheek and lower-mandible contour. Sweep crown plates rearward from a shaped brow and carry the formed cranial surface continuously beneath them. Iteration-03 has a smooth, oversized bill beside a broad cap whose plate seams look irregular; candidate 03 and the July head show a more integrated bill root and compact, swept crown.

2. **Make the optic a recessed feature inside the head.** Preserve the circular amber Advanced optic and visible concentric construction, but seat the lens behind a dark well and fitted raised collar that joins the brow and cheek. The lens should read as glass with depth and a restrained bright center, not a flat disk mounted in a projecting ring. Maker and Mechanic sockets stay passive and dark, per [creative authority](creative-authority.md).

3. **Increase plate density while keeping each plate shaped.** Match the references’ many overlapping, curved, tapered plates around the crown and temple. Use varied lengths and staggered overlaps that follow the skull; keep edges legible and intersections clean. Iteration-03’s broad roof fields and loose seam strips read as a cap laid over the head, while the reference plates form a compact continuous shell.

4. **Join the head to the chest with fitted neck casing.** Candidate 03 shows a compact curved neck with overlapping throat plates flowing into the deep breast. The current close-up has large angular slabs, exposed rails, and gaps that read as unfinished attachment. Add shaped throat, side, and nape casing around the existing frame, retaining deliberate dark mechanical openings and visible articulation boundaries.

5. **Use metal variation to explain construction.** Favor a coherent aged bronze or dark iron base, deep recesses, selective warm edge wear, and a few distinct service or repair pieces. Iteration-03’s mottled surface reads as diffuse noise and competes with the silhouette. Keep patina and wear localized to sheltered areas, exposed edges, and contact points; retain restrained amber emission only for Advanced.

The next visual review can judge these targets from matched head and three-quarter views. This checklist does not score body proportions, structural anatomy, gait, pivot placement, joint range, or movement; those remain with the separate physiology and articulation review. It also does not infer alloy, hidden topology, or fabrication method from the illustrations.
