# V10 Boolean diagnosis

The first unshifted exact Difference is unusable on both sides, and the failure is not explained by open or inconsistently wound input meshes. Direct exact Boolean and the single controlled triangulated-plus-normal-recalculated Boolean both return empty target meshes. No candidate was saved or exported.

The V6 brows are closed, single-component genus-0 meshes (738 vertices, 736 faces, 1,472 evaluated triangles, no boundary/nonmanifold/non-contiguous edges; signed triangle volume about +4.0e−5 m³). Mounts are closed, single-component genus-1 meshes (896 vertices/faces and 1,792 evaluated triangles, no boundary/nonmanifold/non-contiguous edges; signed volumes about +1.55e−4 m³). These signed volumes are descriptive only because the mounts have geometric self-crossings. Normal recalculation did not reverse winding: both signed-volume signs stay positive and close to baseline. The brow self-BVH had no nonadjacent candidate pairs. Mount self-BVH candidates were checked with strict noncoplanar edge-to-face tests that require the crossing point to lie inside the receiving triangle.

The controlled triangulated test triangulated both disposable operands, recalculated face normals, then used Blender’s Exact Boolean Difference. It still returned 0 vertices and 0 faces on both sides, just like the direct untriangulated test. Housing support was absent in both diagnostic outputs. Therefore triangulation and normal repair are not demonstrated remedies. The empty result may arise from the actual crossing surfaces or Blender’s handling of them; this test does not isolate which Boolean failure mechanism applies.

The exact self-crossing check confirms all 17 right and 16 left nonadjacent self-BVH candidate pairs as proper triangle-interior crossings. Crossings occupy world bounds `|X|≈0.099–0.119 m`, `Y≈−0.4531…−0.4332 m`, and `Z≈1.8421…1.8541 m`. Through the retained V3 annular index layout, they map to stations 21–26: outer radial sample `j=6` at stations 21–23 crosses rear-skin samples `j=3…6` at stations 23–26. Triangle and vertex indices, intersection points, and station mapping are in [annular-self-crossing-map-v2.json](annular-self-crossing-map-v2.json).

The original paired-V2 annulus has the `j=0` inner seat at radius about 54.6–55.6 mm around its authored YZ center. That loop is unchanged in V6 to at most 15 nanometres across stations 20–27. Original source radial spans from `j=0` to `j=6` are smooth at about 59.7–60.6 mm. V6’s V3 relief reduces spans at stations 20–23 to 22.9, 28.9, 35.4, and 41.8 mm, then returns abruptly to 59.9 mm at station 24. That abrupt transition aligns with the crossing region. The current data justify preserving the inner seat and rebuilding a smooth, closed upper annular wall around stations 20–27, placing stations on their original YZ radii and solving outer radii against brow clearance. They do not establish a final safe outer width; that must be checked against the preserved brow and optical seat.
