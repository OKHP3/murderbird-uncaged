# Independent visual review: whole-character V15 attempt03

Review basis: matched neutral `before-*` and `after-*` images in this folder; the owner-supplied whole-body target at `context/threads/assets/murderbird-owner-likeness-rejection-2026-09-28/2-Pasted-Image-2.jpg`; Maker reference `assets/img/library/murderbird-unified-maker-clean-candidate-2026-09-06.png`; and Mechanic reference `assets/img/library/murderbird-unified-mechanic-candidate-2026-09-06.png`. The Mechanic image includes a workshop and temporary support rig; those are context, not character anatomy. These are visual comparisons only. The illustrations are not dimensional references, and the neutral renders do not reproduce the references' materials or lighting.

## Visible improvements

- The bill and mandible now read as a hooked avian profile instead of the earlier simple wedge. The cheek and eye assembly has a clearer mechanical surround, and the crown/temporal plates give the skull a more layered silhouette. The after head and profile views are more characterful than their before views.
- The breast no longer exposes large uninterrupted patches of inner shell. Its coverage is substantially fuller, and the staggered rows read as assembled plates. The two side views show a better transition from breast to flank than the previous V15 breast study.
- The lower legs show added bracing and more readable mechanical connections in the after view. This is a construction improvement over the plain, narrow leg members in the before image.

## Regressions and limits

- The breast still reads too broad and generic. In the front view, the 24 plates resolve into a regular rectangular grid with repeated fastener placement. The actual Maker and Mechanic references show a much denser field of small, curved, overlapping feather-shaped plates across the breast and mantle; attempt03's broad rectangular courses lose that scale and hierarchy. The center seam is narrower, but the chest still lacks the references' varied plate contours and dark mechanical depth visible around the body. Coverage improved, while the surface treatment remains a likeness gap.
- The eye is a large blank disk in these neutral views. The owner target's focal orange optic, smaller adjacent sensors, dark facial mask, and dense fine mechanical detail are not represented as visible shape. The upper bill is cleaner and more curved, but remains a broad, smooth light shell compared with the target's segmented, darkly detailed beak.
- The neck remains long and column-like, with stacked plates that read as repeated bands. Its connection into the torso is more constructed, but the target's compact throat-to-breast transition and visible dark linkage are not yet captured.
- The body and stance remain narrower and more tapered toward the lower breast than the target's deep, rounded torso. The shoulder/wing silhouette is smaller and more segmented into mechanical appendages; the target has a fuller scalloped mantle. The after leg view improves bracing, but the long exposed struts and small feet still lack the target's dense joint hardware and broad, heavy clawed-foot presence.
- The actual Maker reference has a compact, rounded body under closely layered breast and wing plates; the Mechanic reference exposes dense internal structure and joint hardware while retaining that same feathered outer silhouette. Attempt03 improves construction detail and surface coverage over its before views, but its torso remains more tapered and its chest plating much larger and more uniform. Its exposed leg rails read as simpler than the dense articulated leg and foot mechanisms visible in both references.

## Likeness judgment

The head silhouette is closer than the before image, especially around the hooked bill and crown, but the large blank optic and smooth bill still omit the actual references' layered facial machinery and smaller secondary optics. Breast coverage is improved, yet its broad, even panel grid is a visible departure from the Maker/Mechanic references' fine feather-like plating. Neck, torso, mantle, and feet proportions are not genuinely closer overall: the model remains longer-necked and more tapered, with a simpler chest and leg silhouette than the references' compact, rounded body and dense layered construction. These conclusions come from the images themselves, not geometry checks or the fact that the regions compose successfully. Human owner likeness acceptance remains pending.
