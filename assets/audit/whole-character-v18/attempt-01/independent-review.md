# Independent whole-bird visual review — V18 attempt 01

Compared the four V18 after views with the camera-matched rejected V10 views in `rejected-v10-matched/` and the owner target `context/threads/assets/murderbird-owner-likeness-rejection-2026-09-28/2-Pasted-Image-2.jpg`. This is a visual comparison of the supplied stills, not an approval or a likeness score. The model renders are monochrome gray; conclusions below concern silhouette and geometry, not color, finish, or material quality.

V18 materially improves the lower legs over rejected V10: the exposed trussed shin structure now reads as a deliberate load-bearing assembly, and the long foot-root/metatarsal span has a formed, tapered guard with visible openings instead of an uninterrupted thin ladder. The new guard gives the legs more section at ordinary full-body scale while keeping the toes and claws visible. The head is also more mechanically articulated around the optic than V10's broad, nearly featureless eye plate, and the swept brow supports create visible structure around the lens.

The main likeness gaps remain conspicuous against the owner target. V18's breast is a large, nearly spherical shield covered in broad, regular scalloped tiles; the target has a tighter compact torso with finer, denser layered plating and visible mechanical depth between shoulder, breast, and neck. The wings read as large flat pointed panels rather than a feathered layered shoulder mass. The neck is short and mostly columnar in these views, losing the target's longer, curved transition from head to breast. The eye still reads as a blank gray disk inside an oversized mechanical surround, while the target's eye is a small, bright focal lens set into a more integrated face. The beak is broad and blocky at its root and the lower bill leaves a conspicuous open slot; the target's hooked profile is slimmer and more continuously integrated with the face.

The front view reinforces the broad, symmetrical chest and plain central breast seam; it does not show the target's narrower, more layered torso. The side view shows the torso-to-neck break and the foreshortened neck more clearly. Relative to rejected V10, the neck is less elongated, but the correction has gone too far toward a straight, compact column. The leg assemblies are visibly stronger than V10, though their joint housings and exposed rods remain thin relative to the target's dense, substantial mechanical legs and ankle/foot roots.

No clear whole-bird regression relative to rejected V10 is visible in these matched views. The lower-leg channel is a useful structural improvement, though its broad guard introduces a more plate-like surface than the target's rounded, layered mechanical construction. The chest's broad tiled treatment and the neck's reduced curve remain the largest silhouette and construction mismatches; the render lighting cannot determine whether other materials would soften their appearance.
