# Head construction findings

Compared the owner-selected target with the scoped July head reference and the unchanged V19/V20 neutral head views. July is used only for head identity; no body proportions are inferred from it.

1. **Turn the bill casing into a layered hooked beak.** Both references show a strong hooked outer contour, but the target's side surface is built from fitted cheek and bridge plates that wrap toward the hook. The current model reads as a clean, smooth casing with a few broad seam lines. Use a shaped brow-to-bridge plate and several irregular side plates that follow the bill's curve, with overlaps and a tapered hook. The plate boundaries should change the edge/profile read, not sit as decorative strips on the same smooth shell.

2. **Give the lower mandible a deliberate owner-state range.** In the owner-selected full-character target, the mouth currently reads comparatively closed, with a narrow dark separation. The July head reference instead shows a substantial open cheek/gape. These are different reference states, not evidence for one fixed mouth opening. The current model's even crescent reads as an unarticulated cartoon smile. Shape a substantial fitted lower mandible and irregular cheek edge that can read close in the owner's selected state while retaining a designed open gape through jaw motion. Preserve the hinge and distal contact; do not solve the silhouette by shifting the whole jaw or suppressing its movement.

3. **Make the optic a recessed bearing assembly, not a goggle.** The model presents a blank, centered disk inside clean concentric rings. The references place a bright circular optic within a deeper, asymmetrically supported socket, with a protective brow/temporal arc and small neighboring mechanisms. Recess the lens behind a nonuniform bearing seat, break the perfect ring with functional supports and open intervals, and integrate its upper edge into the brow and crown plates. Color alone will not correct the goggle-like construction.

The July reference also supports a swept, overlapping crown that wraps toward the temporal/cheek region; the model's repeated rows remain more regular and generic. A few larger directional plates with varied lengths and a clear transition into the socket would help. These are visual construction proposals, not measured dimensions, clearance findings, or owner acceptance. Neutral gray renders limit material and emissive-optic assessment.
