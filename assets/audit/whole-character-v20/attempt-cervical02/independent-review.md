This is a visible form defect, not a claim about fit or articulation.

The owner target's neck is substantial but sweeps through layered, directionally overlapping plates into the breast. cervical02's bands are more horizontal and ring-like, so the form looks mechanically separate from the torso. It is not a convincing improvement over proportions02 and does not establish a useful neck form for movement review.
