I inspected all 42 V8 neutral views against their 42 V7 counterparts, plus the candidate03 full-body, Maker-clean, Mechanic and July owner-selected head images. It retains the V7 neck. These are neutral Workbench renders without textures; source illustrations are qualitative, perspective references rather than measured views.

Across Maker, Mechanic and Builder, V8 keeps the broad upright, heavy-breasted bird silhouette, folded lateral mantle, hooked bill and articulated legs. Head V2 makes the brow, cheek and upper-bill contour read as a more continuous plate assembly than V7. Shoulder attempt02 lowers and tucks the cap modestly; the row-0 fasteners now sit with their plates. Digit attempt03 adds dorsal coverage over the former rounded links. The matched views show no unexpected whole-body silhouette regression from V7; the changed regions remain localized.

The defining mismatch remains the neck-to-breast transition. In front, both profiles, rear and close-up, the unchanged neck reads as a tall, narrow, openly jointed stack with angular struts and a collar break. Candidate03 and the Maker-clean image show a fuller forward sweep into the breast; the Mechanic image also presents a more integrated neck/chest mass beneath its layered plates. V8's reduced shoulder caps are still two distinct side pods rather than one compact folded mantle integrated across the shoulder. The feet have more coverage than V7, but the new covers are sparse: exposed segmented toe links and projecting claws still dominate compared with the broad, plated toe envelope in candidate03. These unresolved silhouette differences are visible in all three neutral eras.

- **Maker:** partial likeness. The heavy torso, folded wing and upright avian stance are retained, and the head/shoulder/foot regions improve over V7. The narrow jointed neck and pod-like caps remain the main departure from Maker-clean.
- **Mechanic:** partial likeness. The same body silhouette fits the reference's mechanical bird direction, but the neutral model remains more sparsely articulated and plated than the dense layered neck, breast and limb machinery visible in the contextual Mechanic illustration. The illustration includes a work setting and human mechanic, so those scene elements are not treated as model requirements.
- **Builder / advanced mode:** partial likeness. Candidate03 supports body and foot comparison; the July owner-selected image is used for head only. V8's smoother brow and hooked bill improve the head contour, while the head still reads as compact plates over a circular optic and the long open neck remains unlike the fuller illustrated head-to-chest transition. No body proportions are inferred from the July head crop.

This evidence supports showing V8 as a complete **review candidate**, with the neck, shoulder integration and toe coverage called out. It does not establish owner acceptance, artistic completion, runtime appearance, motion clearance, material/color fidelity or publication readiness. No score or era acceptance is assigned.
