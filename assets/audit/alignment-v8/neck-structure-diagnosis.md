# V8 neck structure diagnosis

This read-only inspection is bound to V8 native `b12c442e…2478` and its GLB `c8c30cc4…4385`. Blender 5.2.1 read each native mesh's world-space vertex bounds; the existing `builder-side-left.png` was inspected visually. Exact input hashes, full bounds and limitations are in [neck-structure-diagnosis.json](neck-structure-diagnosis.json).

The tall open side outline is formed primarily by `Bowed passive cervical fork` and `.001`, both parented to `neck`. Their respective Blender XYZ bounds are `[-0.12494,-0.28606,1.26692]–[-0.04309,-0.06190,1.66201]` and `[0.04309,-0.28606,1.26692]–[0.12494,-0.06190,1.66201]` metres. They span almost the full visible neck height and read as narrow bent rails. `Cervical articulated inner guards`, also neck-parented, sit behind/between the rails and fill a smaller central volume (`[-0.16195,-0.38828,1.25]–[0.16195,-0.20190,1.65]`). The six `Throat formed lamina` plates descend the anterior face from z 1.644 to 1.238 m; they create a cascade but do not close the side silhouette. All are neck-region frame/plate meshes in the V8 inventory. There is no separate cervical mesh tagged as a bearing; the neck and head controls are empties at world positions `(0,-0.1,1.26)` and `(0,-0.335,1.638)`, respectively, with head parented to neck.

The current runtime bends the neck and counter-rotates the skull at fixed length during strike/contact. A first low-risk construction study could add a fuller curved series of rigid, overlapping plates attached to the existing neck owner, then check them against the head, jaw, breast/fixings, and actual runtime poses. Candidate03 supports a fuller curved neck-to-breast exterior qualitatively; it provides no measurable dimensions, hidden-side topology, or mechanism design. Added interior ribs, bearings, or a different pivot hierarchy would be invented structure unless supported elsewhere. A hierarchy change is not barred by the original contract, but it has wider consequences for motion, contact, inspection restoration, export and pose clearance, and these findings do not establish that it is necessary.

The earlier six-guard study's suspected 100 mm aft-coordinate bug is not present in its current source: it constructs world-space envelope points, transforms each through `neck.matrix_world.inverted()`, and then parents the mesh with identity transforms. That cancels the neck pivot's `-0.1 m` Y translation. The section-center stations also match those in `alignment-v6-neck.py`. Its three separated vertical bands and restricted angular span do not make a broad continuous side construction, so a failed fit does not by itself implicate the pivot hierarchy.

This is an object-bounds and still-image diagnosis only. It does not measure pose clearance, validate collision-free articulation, establish a mechanism from the reference, or accept the geometry artistically.
