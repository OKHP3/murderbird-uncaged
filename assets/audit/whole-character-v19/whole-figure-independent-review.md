# Independent whole-figure shape review — V19 regional attempts

Compared the owner-resupplied target with V19 head03 `after-reference-angle.png` / `after-head.png` and torso04 `after-reference-angle.png` / `after-breast.png`. These are separate regional attempts, not one integrated V19 render, so conclusions describe each visible region and do not claim how an unbuilt combined version would read. The model stills are monochrome gray; this review concerns form and silhouette, not material color, glow, or surface finish. The owner target governs whole-character recognition; the July reference controls head identity only.

The three largest remaining shape/construction gaps are:

1. **Head and mouth read as a generic mechanical raptor rather than this bird.** Head03 improves the upper-bill construction with a curved side panel and narrows the gape, but the bill remains a broad, smooth shell with a strong seam and the lower mandible still leaves a long smile-like opening. The target has a more integrated hooked upper contour, distinct side armor around the cheek, and a narrow, irregular dark slit between upper and lower bill. The target optic is a compact bright focal point; head03's large gray disk is visually blank. Because the test image is gray, the missing glow/color cannot be treated as a shape failure, but the surrounding bill/cheek form still needs work.

2. **The head-to-breast transition loses the target's curved neck.** In head03, the short, mostly upright neck terminates at a hard layered collar above a broad torso. Torso04 adds chest shaping, but its breast closeup still reads as two heavy plated bulges beneath a conspicuous rim; it does not produce the target's continuous curved rise from breast through plated neck into the head. This is a major silhouette relationship, not a surface-detail issue.

3. **Shoulder/wing and leg construction remain too slab-like above and too slight below.** The target shoulder is a deep mass of individually swept overlapping plates, integrated into visible machinery; the model's folded wing remains a broad pointed blanket of repeated broad plates. The target's legs have substantial, dense bearing and link structure from hip through ankle, while the model still reads as narrow rods and exposed pivots beneath its large torso. The V18/V19 leg work improves local section, but it does not yet change the full-body mass relationship enough.

Some local convergence is visible: head03's mandible is closer to the target's narrow open mouth relationship, and torso04 experiments with more shaped breast structure. The current direction is not yet plausibly converged as a whole figure. Larger silhouette corrections remain necessary for the neck curve and leg/shoulder mass relationships; a wholesale head resize is not the first need. The highest-value head work is bill/cheek integration and gape shape. These are independent visual judgments, not owner acceptance or a likeness score.
