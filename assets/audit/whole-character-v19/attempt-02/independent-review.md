# Independent whole-figure review — V19 attempt 02

Compared the final after-reference-angle, after-head, and after-breast stills with the owner-resupplied target. The stills are neutral gray, so this is a shape/construction judgment; gray surfaces do not show whether the eye glow or finished material language is present. The result is an independent visual review, not owner acceptance.

The breast correction is a modest improvement over the earlier bulged torso: the front contour is less interrupted by paired protrusions and the shell reads more continuous. The head retains a more formed bill and a tighter jaw relationship than V18. These are local gains, but the combined bird still reads more like a generic armored mechanical raptor than the specific target.

The three largest remaining discrepancies are:

1. **Neck and torso flow:** the head sits on a short, upright stack that ends at a hard shelf/collar above the chest. The target's curved plated neck flows visibly into the breast. The torso05 silhouette is less lumpy, but still broad and barrel-like; its overlapping breast courses remain more regular and tile-like than the target's layered, mechanically recessed torso.
2. **Bill, cheek, and eye relationship:** the bill is still a broad, smooth casing, and the open lower-bill gap reads as a wide smile from the three-quarter view. The target's deep hook, armored cheek transition, and narrower irregular dark opening are more integrated. The gray optic cannot establish the target's orange glow, while its surrounding shape still looks like a large exposed circular disk.
3. **Shoulder/wing and legs:** the folded wing reads as a broad plated blanket rather than the target's layered shoulder mass with visible mechanical depth. Legs remain too fine and rod-like relative to the large body; the target shows substantially denser bearing, link, and foot-root structure.

The direction shows local construction progress, especially at the breast contour and upper-bill shell, but it is not yet plausibly converged as a recognizable whole figure. Further silhouette-level correction remains necessary, particularly the curved neck-to-breast transition and the relative mass of the leg/shoulder assemblies. A wholesale head resize is not indicated by these views; the remaining head work is in bill/cheek/gape construction and optic integration. This is a visual judgment only.
