## Visible construction cues

**Breast.** In the owner, Candidate03, and Maker images, the breast is rounded, but its surface is not one smooth shell or a few broad horizontal slabs. It has many individual, curved, overlapping plates. The upper chest transitions from the throat and shoulder into a denser breast field; plate edges follow the rounded torso, and the outer field wraps toward the flank and folded wing. Plate sizes and edges vary. Small fasteners, circular joints, and exposed rods or coiled members are visible at selected transitions. Their exact mechanical roles are **unknown** from these images.

V16 has a useful fuller body mass, but its front view is broad and squared. A few large, nearly horizontal rows dominate the breast, with regular plate size and cadence. **Proposal:** preserve the rounded V16 torso envelope while reshaping its breast surface into staggered, curved feather-like plates: smaller and more individually legible than the current slabs; overlap down the torso, with courses turning around the flank and sweeping back toward the folded wing. Keep a clear center breast flow and a tighter transition under the throat. Use nonuniform plate widths, lengths, and edge shapes. Put narrow service seams and fasteners only at intentional plate joints; expose visible understructure at specific access gaps rather than substituting broad bare shell for unresolved surface. Do not add bulk to solve a panel-design problem.

**Face and crown.** The owner, Candidate03, Maker, and July head image show a strong hooked upper bill, a distinct lower bill separated by a dark open mouth line, and a crown made from overlapping plates that sweep back. The side of the face has a prominent round, bright eye-like element in nested circular surrounds; the owner and Candidate03 images also show two smaller round details behind/above the main eye. Curved brow and cheek plates frame the eye and continue toward the bill. Their visible shapes support a focal eye assembly, rings, plate joints, and small circular hardware. Whether the smaller round elements are sensors, bearings, lamps, or something else is **unknown**. Do not invent those functions or add hidden mechanisms based on the pictures.

V16 reduces the empty orbital field compared with attempt02, but the eye remains a plain circular disk on a broad, smooth plate. The crown still reads as tidy repeated rows, and the broad smooth bill and separate lower arc do not yet carry the source's layered, repaired face character. **Proposal:** make the circular optic the face's focal feature by fitting a sculpted brow/cheek closely around its outer ring; turn the remaining orbital plate into shaped overlapping segments rather than an empty flat badge. Preserve the main circular eye and make the smaller visible round elements distinct where they appear in the selected authority views, without assigning unsupported function. Let staggered, tapered crown plates sweep rearward over the skull. Keep the upper bill clearly hooked and the lower bill distinct with a visible mouth gap.
