## Finding

**The specified breast and face goals are not yet met.** Attempt01 adds visible breast segmentation, but the front and breast closeups still read as a field of broad, rectangular armor tiles. The repeated horizontal courses and mostly similar plate proportions dominate; their edges do not yet create the varied, curved, overlapping feather flow seen on the owner, Candidate03, and Maker birds. The reference-angle shows a few more transitions than V16, but they still read as a tiled breastplate. This is a surface-language failure more than a shortage of parts. The new field does not materially change the front silhouette, which remains wide and squared across the chest.

The denser segmentation also makes the repeated front grid more conspicuous, reinforcing the squared chest instead of improving its silhouette. For the next breast pass, preserve the useful rounded side mass and avoid adding bulk. Break the grid into smaller, tapered plates with visibly different widths and shaped lower edges. Let overlapping courses follow the convex breast downward and turn around the flanks toward the folded wing; vary the sweep instead of aligning every seam into level rows. Keep seams narrow and local, and do not leave broad bare shell between plates. The check is whether the front and reference-angle show an armored feather field rather than rectangles laid over a torso.

The face change is visible in the head closeup as additional rings and small rim details around the main optic. In the full-character view it remains subtle. The result still presents a smooth circular disk inside a broad, smooth orbital plate; the visible eye does not gain the focal hierarchy or secondary round details shown behind/above the main eye in the owner and Candidate03 images. The brow and cheek remain largely badge-like instead of framing nested optical forms. The hooked bill and separate lower bill remain readable, but the skull crown still uses orderly repeated plates rather than the backward-swept, varied layering in the references.

For the next face pass, keep the primary circular eye-like feature as the focal point and shape brow and cheek plates closely around its rings to remove the empty badge field. Make the smaller circular forms visible where they appear in the reference images, but do not assign them sensor, bearing, or other functions based on appearance alone. Give the crown plate edges a staggered backward sweep; preserve the hooked upper bill and mouth gap. The check is that the face reads as a layered eye-and-bill assembly in both the head closeup and reference-angle, not just as a plain disk with a thicker ring.

## Decision

**Hold for another visual iteration.** Attempt01 changes the breast into more pieces and adds optic rings, but those changes do not yet deliver the requested plate flow or a convincing focal face. The current render also gives no evidence about the unseen mechanical role of the small round features. Recheck only after another candidate is rendered from the same cameras beside these authority images. No likeness or engineering acceptance is implied.
