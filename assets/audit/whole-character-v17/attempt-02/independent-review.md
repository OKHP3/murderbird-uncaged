# Independent visual review — whole-character V17 attempt 02

**Scope.** I reviewed the six matched before/after neutral renders in this folder against the owner-supplied target, Candidate03, Maker, Mechanic, and July head reference. The native reviewed is `assets/models/whole-character-v17/attempt-02/murderbird-whole-character-v17.blend` (SHA-256 `7d371907b279eb3d625e67164d84a78b2901a5cb6a79917def07f48ff8792d7b`). July is treated as head-only authority. This is a visual comparison, not owner acceptance or a technical clearance result.

**The breast is a clear improvement over V16 and attempt 01.** The dense fitted courses follow the rounded breast envelope in front and three-quarter views, and the smaller staggered plates read as overlapping armor instead of broad rectangular tiles. The baked contour also removes most of the conspicuous bare shell from the prior study. The result is closer to the layered Maker/Mechanic breast treatment. The remaining weakness is cadence: the plates still repeat a similar scallop and scale across much of the chest, so the field reads as a regular manufactured pattern rather than the source's more varied, directional construction. From the side the lower torso remains a large smooth-backed egg beneath that surface; the plate field improves its finish more than its overall proportion. No obvious new silhouette defect appears in the rear view.

**The eye assembly is more legible, but the new supports only partly solve the face.** In the close head and front views, tapered arcs now frame the main lens with visible open intervals; this gives the eye a more deliberate bearing/support structure than the largely blank annulus in the before render. The supports remain secondary to a single large, plain circular disk. The smaller round mechanisms visible above and behind the principal eye in the owner, Candidate03, and July head references are not independently readable in these renders: the crown/temporal plates obscure that area, leaving only small fastener-like details around the main eye. The arcs also appear as several separate brackets in three-quarter view, rather than a fully integrated optical assembly. This is a visual limitation, not a finding about their physical fit.

**Remaining likeness gaps are still substantial.** The head retains the hooked bill and layered crown, but the crown is smoother and more evenly tiled than the source, and the face lacks its smaller clustered machinery and dark recessed structure. The neck and throat remain a relatively clean plated column; the references show a more curved transition with exposed dense mechanism. The breast is fuller and better surfaced, yet the overall body still reads as a broad, egg-shaped shell with sparse visible internal structure, especially from the side. These neutral clay views cannot establish material, illumination, or color similarity.

**Assessment.** Attempt 02 materially improves breast construction and makes the primary eye support more purposeful. It does not make the smaller eye hardware legible, and the regular breast pattern and smooth bulky torso remain notable differences from the owner and Maker/Mechanic images. The change is directionally useful, but the renders do not support a claim of likeness acceptance.
