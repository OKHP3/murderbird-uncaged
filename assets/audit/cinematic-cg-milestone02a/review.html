What this pass changes

Thin bent feather plates replace padded interiors. Smooth sheet normals retain hard rims, dark underlaps and sparse rivets. Recessed cheek apertures reveal inferred gears; curved optical glass covers metallic iris blades and uneven amber coils. Original PBR maps add regional pitting, scratches and interrupted wear across charcoal bronze, steel and machinery. Maker and Mechanic optics remain dark.

The locked artwork remains the acceptance standard. Its dense construction, sharply hooked head and integrated armor are richer than this candidate. Regular shell tiles, a rounded crown and simple tube-and-ball legs still read as stylized; dark breast areas hide too much detail. Improved metal rendering alone is not artistic acceptance. Two valid visual attempts were used; an earlier transform-scale failure was preserved separately and rejected.
