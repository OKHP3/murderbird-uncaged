The generator is [study-cervical-joint-drive-v1.py](../../../../../scripts/study-cervical-joint-drive-v1.py); the full part map and source identity are in [receipt.json](receipt.json).

The load path uses the two inherited upper-owned axles as **separate trunnions**, supported by their corresponding neck-owned clevises. The existing upper cervical rails transfer head load into the upper pivot. The new seven-mesh proposal adds two neck-fixed liner rings, an upper-owned Maker −X sector and cable eye, a neck-fixed Mechanic +X passive brace, and an Advanced +X neck-fixed reaction housing with an upper-owned keyed output collar. The Maker cable route is not built. The Advanced stator is fixed to the neck; only the upper-owned trunnion/output side moves. Mechanic remains passive and locked.

The 21 deterministic Node execution samples of the existing motion/controller modules and 41 app-derived inspection poses were screened with BVH triangle candidates and strict noncoplanar edge-through-face tests. The source packet explicitly does not claim browser rendering or recorded browser frames. Their identities are pinned in [clearance.json](corrected-inspection-sweep/clearance.json). The 41 inspection matrices come from the application’s `applyInspectionPose` and have exact anchor proof in [anchor-proof.json](../../inspection-contract-v1/anchor-proof.json); they are derived samples, not browser captures. The earlier [direct-sweep diagnostic](clearance.json) used an incorrect native breastplate axis and is preserved only as a superseded diagnostic. The subsequent intermediate sweep with a reversed cover translation is preserved under [inspection-sweep-superseded-cover-sign](inspection-sweep-superseded-cover-sign/).

The active Mechanic brace has strict surface crossings against `Cervical flank lamina 1 2`, `Cervical flank lamina 1 3`, `Cervical intermediate axle 1`, `Cervical intermediate clevis 1`, and `Upper cervical curved load rail 1`. In addition, the new `Joint-drive fixed bearing liner +1` intersects the new Mechanic brace. These are actual same-era fit conflicts, so the candidate is held. The exact packet pairs and per-pose data are retained in [clearance.json](corrected-inspection-sweep/clearance.json) and [new-part-interfaces.json](new-part-interfaces.json). The Maker sector/axle and eye/sector, Advanced housing/clevis and output-collar/axle contacts are intended connections; their triangle overlap and strict crossing counts remain visible in the receipts rather than being suppressed.

All seven added mesh objects have zero boundary and non-manifold edges in the topology summary. The original 1,223 scene objects, 52 pivots, curve objects, materials, parent assignments, and rest matrices were preserved by the generation check; no original mesh was altered. The first two drafts, which had a floating Maker sector, are retained under the model and audit `iterations/` folders.
