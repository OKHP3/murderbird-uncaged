# Bill profile study — integration review

Status: preserved proposal; not selected into the v7 limb integration.

The lead reviewed the same-camera before/after three-quarter A images and the actual owner-preferred July reference. The July selection controls the head only. The second view is available in the study receipt but was not part of this lead observation.

The local expansion makes the inner mid-bill contour a little fuller while retaining the opening and terminal point in this static view. It does not resolve the more consequential difference: the reference has a constructed bill root, a distinct cheek-to-bill boundary, and a hierarchy of overlapping hard surfaces. The study still reads as a continuous smooth hook attached to a broad plain orbital field. Accepting the small contour change as a completed head correction would overstate its effect.

Keep the study editable and separate. A subsequent head proposal should address the construction at the bill root and its relationship to the brow and cheek without merely drawing seams onto this surface. It must retain the verified jaw pivot, optic seat, and neck clearance; any altered geometry requires fresh jaw/head, jaw/neck, optic, and strike-contact checks.

This is a qualitative comparison of differently lit perspective imagery, not dimensional measurement or owner artistic acceptance. No runtime model was changed by this review.
