What changed, and what remains

Accepted 1C proportions are retained. Bounded curved interpolation rounds torso and folded-shield section transitions, moving plates and backing together. A light subdivision pass softens the skull/bill cage faceting and existing head/neck plate faces. Foot and leg geometry, joint positions and materials are preserved. The power/transmission counterweight remains fictional, with no balance certification.

The visible gain is modest: rounder upper shoulder caps and smoother body taper, rather than a new silhouette. Plate separation, the flat optic, cheek machinery and broad-shell appearance remain surface problems for the proposed Milestone 3. No final likeness acceptance is claimed.
