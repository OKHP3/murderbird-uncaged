## Highest-impact proportional edits

1. **Shorten the visible neck transition modestly while keeping its curve.** V15 separates skull and shoulders with a long, narrow plated column. In the references the skull still flows through a clear forward throat curve, but the lower neck meets the breast sooner and flares into it. Shorten roughly the lower fifth of the current visible neck interval, primarily by raising/rounding the shoulder-to-throat transition. Preserve the curved back-of-neck and forward throat sweep; simply deleting the neck or making it straight would erase the bird's identity.

2. **Give the torso more vertical body mass and a rounder breast profile without scaling it into a barrel.** In rough side/three-quarter crops, the reference torso from shoulder line to belly spans about 0.38–0.42 of skull-top-to-ground image height; V15 reads closer to about 0.32–0.36. Camera differences make these ranges directional only. Extend fullness through the upper/anterior breast and lower chest, with a rounded belly and a retained narrowing into the hip/leg junction. Keep the back/flank and waist taper comparatively stable; do not widen every torso section uniformly.

3. **Broaden and lower the mantle/shoulder silhouette.** The references carry a broad scalloped wing cover over much of the upper flank. V15's shoulder plates sit as a smaller, higher cap and leave a narrow mechanical body above the leg emergence. Widen the shoulder cover outward and let its outer edge descend farther over the upper flank, while preserving a clear wing-to-body break. Aim for a fuller cap, not a uniformly wider torso.

4. **Reduce the dominance of the blank orbital disk and restore a clustered head silhouette.** V15's continuous cheek plate makes one large circular blank surface the head's strongest feature. The references give the eye a smaller focal lens inside a layered dark socket with nearby secondary optics. Keep the main eye location and hooked-beak contour; reduce the broad uninterrupted disk area and distribute some of its visual width into smaller adjacent optic housings and brow/cheek structure. This is primarily a silhouette hierarchy change, not a material or glow fix.

5. **Increase the apparent joint and foot mass relative to the exposed rails.** The references' knees, ankles, and metatarsal assemblies read as dense articulated mechanisms; V15's long open braces dominate and its feet read light. Keep joint centers and toe pivots fixed, but give the existing bearing regions more visual width/depth and make the metatarsal/claw group occupy more of the foot silhouette. Avoid extending rigid additions across moving joints. The Mechanic support stand must not be copied into the character.

The strongest composition-level gap is still the torso/shoulder mass balance: V15 is a narrow-necked, tapered, plated bird, while the references read as a compact, rounded breast beneath a wide, overlapping mantle. Study04's head and leg construction do not resolve that proportion gap. This is a visual proposal for root's V16 work, not an acceptance judgment or a dimensional specification.
