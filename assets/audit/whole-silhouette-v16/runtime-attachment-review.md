## Attachment classification

The runtime currently mixes three patterns:

- **Rest-rig-derived:** foot origin/ground height, hip/knee/ankle link offsets, leg lengths, actual hip/knee actuator ends, actual shoulder/elbow endpoints, actual power-core source, and bill triangle contact point.
- **Node-derived with fixed local offsets:** Maker cable/rod horns, Builder cervical anchors, Builder bus/manifold and conduit routes, body-local Mechanic spindle/cranks, shoulder midpoint ratio, and the body-relative pelvis support point.
- **Fixed pose/trajectory values, not attachment points:** crouch/bob, pitch splits and maxima, step/claw/jump distances and lifts, support margin, rail contact plane, and gait timing.

The most geometry-sensitive hard-coded attachments for V16 are the cervical line/actuator offsets, Mechanic spindle and cross-shaft span, hip/foot trajectory amplitudes, pelvis brace bases, and the fixed bill-to-rail contact plane. Shoulder endpoints and the leg solver’s segment lengths already follow the loaded pivots, but still require fresh clearance and range review.
