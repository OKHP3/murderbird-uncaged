## Assessment

Attempt03 retains the useful V16 change: the fuller chest and shoulder transition, deeper body mass, and slightly stronger foot stance read closer to the rounded, weight-bearing character in the owner and Maker references than V15's slimmer form. The silhouette has not become a uniform barrel: the front narrows below the breast and the side still tapers toward the hips. However, the wider front view now reads as a broad squared armor barrel, so the added mass is only a partial likeness gain. The body surface is still the larger problem: a few large, regular horizontal plate courses replace the references' dense, small, curved feather-like layers. The plates need greater variation, overlap, and direction to read as feather armor instead of generic torso plating.

The attempt03 neck edit is a small visible improvement over attempt02: the head/neck transition is less crowded, while the forward throat sweep and curved rear contour remain. It has not lost the curved-neck identity. The neck remains long and visually slender against the enlarged chest, and the lower guard courses still bunch where the neck meets the breast. Preserve the curve; improve the lower transition and proportion with the breast rather than shortening the neck again.

The orbital-field edit is visible compared with attempt02: the broad eye plate is tighter around the optic. It remains a mostly blank, smooth surround around a plain circular disk. The owner target has a much stronger focal eye assembly, with a bright inset and layered mechanical detail. The head still lacks that visual hierarchy; the smaller housing alone does not complete the eye. The crown's orderly plate rows and the smooth wedge-like bill also remain cleaner and more regular than the source's rougher, feathered and repaired construction.

The wing/mantle is still a compact cap relative to the broad folded cover in the owner, Candidate03, and Maker images. The legs read as articulated and the enlarged feet improve support, but the lower limbs and toes remain thin and exposed beside the references' heavier, denser joints and clawed feet. The Mechanic image supports visible serviceable structure, but this model's repeated exposed trusses do not yet supply the same mass or surface character.

## Decision and remaining gaps

**Hold as a working proposal.** Attempt03 is directionally better than V15 in torso mass and stance, and the eye/neck cleanup is modestly visible. It is not yet a convincing likeness. Highest-impact remaining work is (1) develop a more varied, curved, overlapping breast/feather field while retaining the improved body silhouette; (2) build a distinct layered optic and crown treatment rather than relying on a reduced blank plate; and (3) broaden the folded mantle and strengthen the lower-leg/foot visual mass. These are artistic judgments, not proof of clearance or construction validity. No owner acceptance is recorded.
