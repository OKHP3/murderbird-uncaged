## Finding

The V16 silhouette is materially closer at the whole-body level: the torso has more of the rounded chest mass seen in the references, the shoulder/body transition is fuller, and the stance feels better supported. It has not become a uniform barrel. The front still narrows toward the lower abdomen/hip, and the side remains an egg-shaped breast profile. The breast plates themselves remain broad and regular, so the references' dense, small, curved feather-like scale field is still missing.

The neck is shorter, but it has not lost its curved identity. The side view retains a forward throat sweep and curved rear contour. The regression is at the neck base: overlapping guard plates bunch into a crowded, uneven collar where the neck meets the breast. Re-space or reshape that short transition before runtime work; preserve the current neck centerline and its curve rather than shortening the neck again.

The smaller optic does not solve the eye treatment. The broad orbital plate stays almost the same size, so shrinking the lens leaves a larger blank annulus/field around it. In the head view, the eye reads less as a focal mechanical cluster and more as a small inset on a large empty plate. A targeted correction should reduce/reprofile the flat plate around the lens and let the brow/cheek carry the surround, with smaller secondary optical forms restored as visible geometry. This is the clearest head-region regression in attempt02.

The shoulder and body mass now better match the rounded reference silhouette, but the mantle still reads as a narrow cap compared with the broad scalloped wing cover in the source images. The leg stance is stronger; exposed braces and the relatively light foot assemblies still look simpler than the references' dense joints and heavy clawed feet. Neutral render views show geometry only and do not assess final materials, runtime poses, clearance, or owner acceptance.

**Immediate correction before runtime work:** correct the blank orbital field left by reducing the optic, and smooth the bunched lower-neck plate transition without undoing the retained curve. Keep the fuller torso; no broad body rollback is indicated by these views.
