# Architect assessment of the source-aligned CG job

The model remains short of the owner’s source-alignment target. Full09 supplies a complete technical packet for an editable three-era development study, and the independent Equilibrium decision rejects treating it as a completed photoreal visual twin. The study preserves useful localized gains and exposes their competing regressions. No region is automatically accepted merely because its worker or local QC retained it for an integration trial.

The principal failure is the construction representation. The source has a compact, densely layered mechanical character; the candidate still uses a broad smooth crown and bill, sparse cheek bars, elongated smooth breast courses and simplified feet. Current source, head/detail, eight-view and desktop browser images show these differences. The conditional disruptor tested camera, lighting and display scale as a sole explanation and did not overturn the construction finding. Exact source depth, rear shape and compactness measurements remain uncertain.

A second limit is source registration against the inherited stance. A fresh read-only reprojection of the actual saved attempt09 anchors under the existing camera04 ortho-35 comparison gives horizontal knee spacing of 83.15 px versus 230 px in existing source annotations, and horizontal ankle spacing of 108.55 px versus 217 px. The optic and bill-hook picks remain roughly 104 px and 81 px left of their source picks. These are camera-projected annotation comparisons, not calibrated source geometry or a prescription for physical joint displacement. Broad breast/head contours were not measured by those point annotations. Approved stance anchors remain intact; an alternate pose cannot be implied by the remaining residuals.

The process also spent too many iterations making localized constructions admissible before a coupled whole-character result was demonstrated. Precise point fits, optical-ray clearance, UV bounds and preservation receipts are important safeguards, but a new worker name or a new guide parameter does not establish a new construction approach or a visible source gain. CHEEK19 stopped after its first whole render and fresh QC rejected its smooth tube/gold sheet. The attempt09 review keeps net whole-character gain and blanket module retention open.
