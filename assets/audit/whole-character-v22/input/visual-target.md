# V22 structural remap visual target

Compared the owner whole-bird reference with the retained V21 construction06 three-quarter, front, and side views. The reference is a perspective illustration with dramatic lighting; these observations describe visible relationships, not calibrated dimensions.

- **Neck and head path:** The reference has a long, readable S-curve: the head and hooked bill project forward, the throat descends and turns into the breast, while layered nape armor continues back over the shoulder. Construction06 compresses this into a short, nearly vertical neck column between a large head and egg-shaped torso. Preserve a curved throat-to-chest transition and rearward nape line; avoid solving the gap with another straight collar or ring stack.
- **Head versus bill:** In the reference, the eye sits inside a compact mechanical brow/socket and the long hooked bill is a distinct forward mass below it. Construction06 gives the head a broad smooth cap and the bill reads as a large plain shell; its head-to-neck join also appears abrupt. Keep the forward bill projection legible, but stage the eye and brow within the head silhouette and make the head sit naturally at the top of the curved neck. July material is head-only; this whole-bird target controls the body relationship.
- **Breast, shoulder, and pelvis:** The reference’s breast is full and forward, then narrows toward a visible underside/leg-root structure; it does not read as one uninterrupted egg. Its compact layered shoulder mantle laps into the neck and chest. Construction06 has a smooth barrel-like torso, with bilateral shields separated from the breast by exposed supports and a broad blank body field. The structural remap should establish one coherent chest-to-shoulder envelope, then taper into a visible pelvic/leg-root frame rather than enlarging the barrel.
- **Leg exposure and grounding:** The reference leaves the long load path visible below the breast: articulated upper legs, bearing transitions, substantial paired members, and broad multi-toed feet. Construction06 exposes its legs too, but they read as thin repeated bars and oversized round joints beneath the heavy torso. Increase the leg assemblies’ structural presence in relation to the chest, retain distinct knee/ankle transitions, and let the feet ground the stance without enlarging them into boots.

These are silhouette priorities for the rest remap, not a claim that V21 is accepted or that perspective artwork supplies exact physical dimensions.
