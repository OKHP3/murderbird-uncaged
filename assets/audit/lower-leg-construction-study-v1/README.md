# Lower-leg construction study V1

This is one editable, add-only neutral native proposal based on Alignment V8, prepared for early visual review. It adds paired closed-section load spars, proximal and distal cross-saddles, and a partial front guard with an open inspection window and diagonal returns to the spars. The returned guard is physically tied to the proposed spars; it does not attach to an inherited shin sheath.

The source hash is `b12c442e2f517b676cdd1fae1612730cc0ba83251d34f363285b08f2cd482478`. The candidate hash is `415a3aa7976c271c28187eb4ee1440dbcd858f4dfb5b84cb6ed69e381def982f`. The native was authored from ten passive meshes, five parented to each existing shin pivot. No source geometry, pivot, curve, material, or foot/talon object was changed. The independent preservation check is in [supervisor-native-comparison.json](supervisor-native-comparison.json).

The matched rest views use the same fixed camera, scale, Workbench lighting, and era visibility as the V8 neutral views. The exact source view hashes, render script hash, and camera comparison contract are recorded in [render-manifest.json](render-manifest.json). Candidate renders are under [renders](renders/); corresponding V8 source images are under `assets/audit/alignment-v8/neutral-views/`. The body profile is [maker side-right](renders/maker-side-right.png), and the close fit is [maker feet-side](renders/maker-feet-side.png). Camera equality supports a fixed-rest comparison; it is not browser or exported appearance proof.

Visual review sees a modest increase in paired-member structure, while the forward window still reads as a stand-off handle. The fit screen also found unresolved intersections. [guard-strict-crossings.json](guard-strict-crossings.json) uses the world-transformed evaluated surface helper at `scripts/diagnose-native-regional-clearance.py` (SHA-256 `dfa270b5bb9175772d1e0665560254914899a20dab9458e4412b237800b85a61`) and the frozen `proper_crossing_receipt` kernel in `assets/audit/cervical-construction-study-v1/attempt-07/actual-runtime-joint-clearance-v1/executed-review.py` (SHA-256 `6d840608cf43b20beccaa8a7f1cab274d000485996ea2b1a6e1460921092b356`). The exact diagnostic source is preserved as [guard-strict-check-executed.py](guard-strict-check-executed.py), SHA-256 `ba9eefebfa904bfab649bfab9bd72ae2eca4baddee079d0d29a21057540fb4f2`; its native input hash and world XYZ convention are recorded in the receipt. The current generator at the same path now has diagnostic-only modes and SHA-256 `ba9eefebfa904bfab649bfab9bd72ae2eca4baddee079d0d29a21057540fb4f2`; the build-time source bytes were not retained, so the current file is not presented as the frozen generator used for the saved native.

Both guards strictly cross their two new spar members, the intended fixed assembly fits. The left guard also strictly crosses the inherited proximal and distal shin plates and two inherited sheath fixings. The right guard strictly crosses two inherited sheath fixings. Those old-part intersections are not accepted by same-owner labeling. There are zero different-owner strict pairs in this guard-only rest-state scope. [guard-triangle-intersection-check.json](guard-triangle-intersection-check.json) independently recomputes two edge-to-triangle hit points for left guard triangle 79 against inherited proximal shin-guard triangle 406. This is a surface intersection receipt, not penetration depth. The earlier [guard-attachment-check.json](guard-attachment-check.json) is retained but explicitly marked invalid because it mixed object-local BVHs and world vertices; do not use its distances or overlaps. [supervisor-frame-audit.json](supervisor-frame-audit.json) confirms the saved scene at frame 1 has matching raw/evaluated bounds and that the distant Short dorsal shell does not overlap the guards.

The captured references are the Candidate03, Maker, and Mechanic raster images listed with hashes in [regional-era-attachment-map.json](regional-era-attachment-map.json). They support an authored visual interpretation, not measured dimensions or hidden construction. The Mechanic service stand is external. Builder and Mechanic procedural drives are described in `src/scene/era-mechanisms.js`; their moving runtime surfaces are not part of this native file, so exact dynamic clearances remain unverified.

Available pose data has limits. `assets/audit/neutral-runtime-clearance-poses-v1/pose-snapshot.json` (SHA-256 `874396ede48a63d37d743a1a85ae885a10e614a4461f46c50f3a62744a9e2813`) is a V7 packet whose raw-rest pivot matrices are preserved in V8. It is not a captured V8 runtime pose packet and contains no exact step, crouch, landing, or claw transform rows. V8 browser telemetry and motion video are hash-recorded in the map, but they do not provide per-frame pivot matrices, and this study was not exported into the browser runtime. Consequently this V1 is held as an early native visual/fit proposal, not a checked six-pose result, engineering validation, or F04 likeness closure. Containment, swept clearance, contact loading, and dynamic drive clearance are not proven. Foot and talon likeness remain separately open.
