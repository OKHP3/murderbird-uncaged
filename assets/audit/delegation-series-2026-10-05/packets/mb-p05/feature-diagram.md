## Landmark contract

| Landmark | July-source cue and candidate test | Ownership boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Crown sweep | Crown begins behind/above the optic and sweeps back in several overlapping, tapering plates. Avoid a smooth oversized helmet, uniform barrel bands, or a long sparse crest. Check front, profile, opposite, rear, high and low views. | `MBH01 Crown Sweep`; overlaps are part of the head. |
| Brow and optic | The brow projects over a circular optic housing; the lens is visibly recessed inside concentric mechanical rings, not a flat eye decal. Keep relative optic scale and severity. Assign era-specific optic state only from the matching era source. | `MBH01 Brow Bridge`, `MBH01 Recessed Optic Housing`; geometry shared, material response per era. |
| Upper bill | Start from a substantial root below/in front of the optic; maintain deep convex volume and an unmistakable long downward hook to the tip. Reject a flat wedge, thin arc, cute rounded beak, or drooping snout. | `MBH01 Upper Bill`; one coherent silhouette from root to tip. |
| Cheek opening | Preserve a readable dark negative-space opening between bill and cheek/jaw, with a mechanical rim and visible depth. Avoid a filled cheek shell that merges into the bill. | `MBH01 Cheek Rim`; the open region is an evaluated gap, not a solid fill object. |
| Lower mandible | Keep a distinct lower-jaw edge and tip beneath the upper bill. A static open-pose comparison is not motion evidence. | `MBH01 Lower Mandible`; no rig or movement scope. |
| Head/neck seam | Head meets the retained curved plated neck at one intentional joining lip; show clearance below the bill and avoid a hidden/straight stacked neck. Keep edits above the seam. | `MBH01 Head Neck Interface`; existing neck below seam is preserved. |
