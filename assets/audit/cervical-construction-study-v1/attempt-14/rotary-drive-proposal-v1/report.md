# Joint-centered rotary-drive proposal

Attempt 14’s upper pitch is about the `cervical-upper` pivot’s local +X axis. A single compact coaxial joint package is worth testing in a native prototype: keep the shared pivot passive, support an upper-frame shaft in two neck-fixed bearing seats at the existing left/right `Cervical intermediate clevis` members, and connect that shaft to the upper frame through its paired `Upper cervical curved load rail` members. The load path would run from head and upper rails, through the keyed shaft and bearing seats, into the lower neck frame. An Advanced motor’s reaction housing would attach to the fixed neck-side clevis bridge; a motor floating at the center would have no supported reaction path.

This is a topology proposal; runtime capability and geometry remain unchanged.
