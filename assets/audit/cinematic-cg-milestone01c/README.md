## Scope and result

Study 05 is the geometry control: elongated pointed torso, embedded avian hips,
short thick neck, softened rear skull and enlarged folded shields. Its source
binary is preserved. Mechanical detail was rebuilt around this scaffold. The
posterior low power/transmission mass remains a fictional proposal, not a
validated assembly. The paper's terrestrial-bird comparison informs mass-position
rationale; it cannot prove stability from silhouette.

Two integrated attempts were made. Attempt 01 exposed armor hidden by solid
backing. Attempt 02 recessed hidden backing by 10% about section centres and
improved curved plate surfaces and orbital detail. Outer scaffold section anchors,
head size/location and joint positions were retained. Added millimetre-scale plate
skins, edge pieces and recurved talon surfaces are visual additions. Hidden regions
are inferred; intersections are allowed for this CG study.

**Remaining failures:** the breast and shields still read as broad shallow shells;
plate seams are too regular; shoulders are crude; the optic is flat and the cheek
lacks the source's dense machinery. Workshop lighting loses lower-body detail.
This is progress in stance and regional construction, not an accepted visual twin.
Do not interpret the two-attempt checkpoint as completion of the CG track.
