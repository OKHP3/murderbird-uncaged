What changed, and what remains

Study 05 controls the elongated tapered torso, embedded hips, shortened thick neck, softened rear skull and enlarged folded shields. Added features include overlapping crown plates, neck armour, orbital rings, segmented hooked bill, leg pistons, joints and recurved talons. Heavy components low and behind the hip remain a fictional counterweight proposal.

Remaining failures: breast and wing plating reads as broad shallow shells with overly regular seams; shoulder joins are crude; the optic is too flat; the crown and cheek lack the reference’s dense mechanism detail. Warm lighting hides too much of the lower torso. These differences prevent a likeness claim.
