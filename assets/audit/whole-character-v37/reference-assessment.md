# V37 reference assessment

The controlling source for whole-body form is the owner-supplied target at [`2-Pasted-Image-2.jpg`](../../../context/threads/assets/murderbird-owner-likeness-rejection-2026-09-28/2-Pasted-Image-2.jpg). The V35 and V36 reference-angle renders use matched cameras; the target is a dramatic perspective image, so the comparisons below are qualitative rather than dimensional. The July selection is scoped to head identity only and does not set body proportions.

1. **Breast, pelvis, and shoulder do not read as one heavy body.** In the owner image, the broad breast transitions into a compact lower body and the hip roots sit under the mantle. In both neutral candidates, the breast ends in a narrow pointed bib above a conspicuous horizontal hip axle; the shoulder caps sit outboard as separate pods with open triangular gaps at their inner roots. A noticeable correction would carry the breast's lower mass into a compact pelvis, tuck the axle visually under that mass, and connect each mantle root into the nape and torso side while retaining the dark working space beneath the folded wing. Do not fill the opening with a featureless shell.

2. **The head assembly remains mask-like despite V36's new optic opening.** The target's bill, brow, cheek, and swept crown form a continuous hooked profile, with the large optic nested among diagonal plates and smaller visible fittings. V35 has a blank circular socket face; V36 exposes the internal fittings, but the angular dark aperture and broad flat cheek still make the eye look mounted on a mask, and the bill remains a blunt wedge. A meaningful correction would reshape the brow-to-cheek envelope around the optic and restore a more deeply curved bill return, keeping the crown swept back rather than flattening it into a cap.

3. **The leg chain is too lightly supported at the hip, ankle, and toe root.** The candidates show exposed circular joints connected by narrow hanging links, then long thin toe rails and needle-like claws. The reference has denser mechanical sections through the thighs and shanks, a substantial ankle/metatarsal transition, and short, visibly curved talons. V36 makes the foot more compact than V35, but the planted foot still reads as a sparse fork. A noticeable correction would add real section to the load-bearing members and toe root, with stout curved claws, while preserving the birdlike toe arrangement and visible articulation.

These are silhouette and construction observations under neutral shading, not likeness acceptance or clearance findings.
