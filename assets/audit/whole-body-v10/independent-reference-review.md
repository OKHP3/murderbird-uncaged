# Independent silhouette review for unified V10

This review compares Candidate03, Maker-clean, and Mechanic reference images with the matched neutral V9 views. It ranks three visible relationships for the V10 whole-body review. The images are concept references, not dimensional drawings; the guidance below is qualitative and does not claim exact scale or engineering dimensions.

## Ranked relationships to improve

1. **Neck into breast.** Candidate03 and Maker-clean show a substantial, curved throat that carries the head into the chest; the front breast remains full without a pinched neck root. V9’s side view reads as a long, narrow, angular and partly open neck above a large, rounded breast. Bring the throat into the breast with a continuous curved profile and enough visible plate mass to avoid the skeletal-rail reading, while retaining the head’s upright terrorbird posture. Check the side and three-quarter views together so added throat volume does not become a collar.

   Visible reference scope: [Candidate03](../../../img/library/murderbird-unified-master-candidate-03-2026-09-06.png), [Maker-clean](../../../img/library/murderbird-unified-maker-clean-candidate-2026-09-06.png); comparison: [V9 Maker side](../alignment-v9/neutral-views/maker-side-left.png), [V9 Mechanic side](../alignment-v9/neutral-views/mechanic-side-left.png), [V9 selected profile](../supervisor-checkpoint-2026-09-28/selected-v9-profile.png).

2. **Shoulder into body and wing.** Candidate03’s shoulder and layered wing read as one draped lateral mass following the torso. Maker-clean likewise keeps the shoulder transition covered by the wing envelope. In V9’s front and three-quarter views, the exposed, dark shoulder capsules sit outside the torso and interrupt the wing-to-body contour; the wing plates then hang as a separate shell. Blend the shoulder mass into the upper thorax and let the wing envelope cover or transition around that junction, preserving the visible layered wing shape. Do not solve this by broadening the whole torso.

   Visible reference scope: [Candidate03](../../../img/library/murderbird-unified-master-candidate-03-2026-09-06.png), [Maker-clean](../../../img/library/murderbird-unified-maker-clean-candidate-2026-09-06.png), [Mechanic](../../../img/library/murderbird-unified-mechanic-candidate-2026-09-06.png); comparison: [V9 Maker three-quarter](../alignment-v9/neutral-views/maker-three-quarter.png), [V9 Mechanic three-quarter](../alignment-v9/neutral-views/mechanic-three-quarter.png), [V9 Maker front](../alignment-v9/neutral-views/maker-front.png).

3. **Leg mass and body support.** Candidate03 and the Mechanic reference show substantial, articulated lower limbs that visually carry the heavy body; the feet spread into clear grasping toes. V9’s side view makes the upper legs look like narrow exposed struts beneath a large egg-shaped torso, with the round joints drawing more attention than the connecting limb segments. Give the load-bearing segments more continuous armored mass and a clearer transition under the belly; keep joints legible but subordinate to the limb silhouette, and preserve the broad, separated toe/claw footprint.

   Visible reference scope: [Candidate03](../../../img/library/murderbird-unified-master-candidate-03-2026-09-06.png), [Mechanic](../../../img/library/murderbird-unified-mechanic-candidate-2026-09-06.png); comparison: [V9 Maker side](../alignment-v9/neutral-views/maker-side-left.png), [V9 Mechanic side](../alignment-v9/neutral-views/mechanic-side-left.png), [V9 Mechanic three-quarter](../alignment-v9/neutral-views/mechanic-three-quarter.png).

## V10 comparison

Reviewed the matched [V10 three-quarter](attempt-01/three-quarter.png), [profile](attempt-01/profile.png), [front](attempt-01/front.png), and [neck/shoulder closeup](attempt-01/neck-shoulder.png) against the [V9 checkpoint three-quarter](../supervisor-checkpoint-2026-09-28/selected-v9-three-quarter.png), [profile](../supervisor-checkpoint-2026-09-28/selected-v9-profile.png), and [head view](../supervisor-checkpoint-2026-09-28/selected-v9-head.png), plus the same reference images above. V10 native identity is recorded in [attempt-01 receipt](attempt-01/receipt.json), SHA `cdf771490ea39583204fa54883be2995f9178dc41ddd7233cd842709d85635bb`.

1. **Neck into breast — improved, still needs review.** The new throat plates make a visibly continuous curved bridge from head to chest and remove much of V9’s open-rail look. In the three-quarter view, the stacked throat plates still read as a tall sleeve; keep the curved profile but refine how its lower plates merge into the breast rather than adding more volume. No clear silhouette regression against V9.

2. **Shoulder into body and wing — partial improvement, not closed.** Pulling the inner shoulder coverts inward reduces some of the exposed junction, but the front and three-quarter views still show a prominent shoulder pod breaking the torso-to-wing contour. The upper wing remains a separate outer shell. Integrate or cover that junction further while keeping the wing’s lateral span and layered outline. This is not yet a pass, but the sampled views do not show a clear regression versus V9.

3. **Leg mass and body support — reject the current guard shape as a reference regression.** The thicker midsections improve the impression that the legs carry the torso, but the smooth swollen guards make the limbs read softer and more organic than the mechanical, articulated legs in Candidate03 and the Mechanic reference. They also compete with the joints and neighboring struts. Keep some added support mass, but reduce the bulbous profile and restore clearer mechanical segmentation around the joints; do not simply return to V9’s too-thin exposed struts.

This visual review does not establish collision clearance, motion quality, browser behavior, artistic acceptance, or approval. It evaluates the supplied neutral images only.
