# V31 head and face reference contract

## Authority and limits

The owner whole-bird image controls the neutral exterior and closed-mouth expression. The July image is a head-only construction reference; it shows a pronounced open gape and is not evidence for body proportions or for a permanently open neutral jaw. Treat them as two poses of one proposed assembly, not two conflicting mouth-shape targets. The references use different, perspective views from the neutral Form02 renders, so these notes describe visible relationships only; they are not measurements.

Viewed inputs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [reference-head-contract.json](reference-head-contract.json). The Form02 gray images are useful for shape and negative-space review. Their untextured appearance does not establish the final material design.

## Five constraints for the head worker

1. **Build one continuous bird profile.** From the side, the crown flows into a substantial forward brow and deep hooked upper bill; the bill narrows toward a downward-curved tip. The lower mandible must be a distinct curved member with a real hinge behind and below the optic. V30's blunt bill mass and nearly straight lower edge make the face read as a generic mask.
2. **Keep two intentional jaw states.** In the owner target's neutral pose, the mandible follows the upper bill closely, leaving a narrow, irregular dark mouth seam. The July reference's open pose shows a much larger gape and a rising, curved lower jaw, with the mouth cavity visible. Preserve the opening articulation and cavity; do not bake the July gape into the neutral rest or flatten the lower jaw into a straight shelf.
3. **Nest the optic in the face.** The owner image places a bright central lens deep inside concentric bearing/lid structure, with the brow overlapping its upper edge and a cheek return framing the lower/front edge. Keep the aperture as a purposeful dark annulus around the lens. Avoid V30's isolated coin-like disc sitting on a broad, smooth field; the surrounding plates and bearing should visibly wrap the optic in three dimensions.
4. **Make brow, cheek and bill meet diagonally.** The brow sweeps forward and down toward the upper bill; a separate cheek plate runs back from that transition beneath the optic and turns into the jaw hinge. Their edges create a narrow, readable negative-space arc around the eye and mouth. Avoid a broad uninterrupted face plate, a straight horizontal shelf below the eye, and a circular ring that reads as a complete goggle mounted on top.
5. **Carry the crown over and aft.** The owner and July images show overlapping, aft-swept crown/temporal plates wrapping from the forehead over the skull and behind the optic. Keep their edges nested with small shadow breaks and a continuous outer skull contour. V30's broad smooth cap and disconnected-looking plate breaks flatten the skull and separate the eye from the crown. Do not use long fins or leave a large roof opening to manufacture separation.

## Assessment of V30 Form02

Form02 is directionally better in its compact upper-bill contour and tapered chest, but its eye remains a plain round insert in a broad mask-like face. The bill is too blunt, the mandible reads nearly straight, and the crown/cheek/brow do not yet form a continuous wrapped assembly. These are the strongest reasons it still reads as a generic cartoon head rather than the owner's mechanical terrorbird. This is a construction target, not artistic approval or evidence of fit/clearance.
