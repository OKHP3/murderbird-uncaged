## Proposed minimal allowed rebuild contract

**Retain functions, not legacy shapes.** Keep a rigid counter-rotating skull connected to the last cervical hinge; a separately rigid lower jaw opening about a real transverse bearing; a dedicated fixed `upper-bill` surface for contact; an independently opening `cranial-cover`; and era-specific optics/processing. Shared journals, caps, frame, pins, cheek supports and mandible bridge may be replaced, consolidated, resized or reseated to serve the deep hook/curved jaw/crown silhouette. No requirement demands the present jaw coin diameter, forward chin rail, duplicate skull shafts or unchanged jaw pivot. Earlier `BOUNDARY` / “jawHingeUnchanged” assertions in V25–V30 modules were imposed preservation scope, not user art requirements.

For this head-only pass, keep the skull/neck pivot and cervical-upper receivers fixed as a compositional boundary. Rebuild the **head-owned** shaft ends to fit the actual existing ±.071 races, and build a readable head-owned load frame from that bearing into the skull and fixed jaw journals. One rigid part has one owner; do not bridge the moving skull to the neck with one rigid mesh. Consolidating the two overlapping head-owned pins is a proposal, provided the retained bearing passage and axial capture remain explicit. Moving the skull pivot itself is technically possible but expands into neck-link/race endpoint reconstruction and must be coordinated with root; it is not necessary merely to move the jaw.

Move the jaw coherently to an authored point **Q** inside the rebuilt cheek, keeping parent `head`, zero rest orientation, unit scale and transverse local +X. Preserve desired closed-world mandible points while changing the origin: after setting `jaw.matrix_world.translation = Q` and updating the dependency graph, install each moving part's desired world vertices through its **new evaluated inverse world matrix**. Rebuild fixed head-owned journals and moving jaw-owned caps around the same Q/X axis; move their entire axial stacks together, and connect the fixed journals into the new skull frame. Do not merely translate the jaw node while leaving its meshes or fixed coins at the old centre. Journal/race appearance may change; exact centring, finite bearing passage, rigid ownership and real support endpoints remain the functional requirements.

Current controller API assigns `nodes.jaw.rotation.x` directly: Maker **+.32 × input** (`src/scene/era-motion.js:285`), normal Advanced jaw pulse **+.27 × pose.jaw** (`:472`). The actual loader captures authored rest positions from the new GLB. A pivot-only relocation with unchanged local X and zero rest rotation requires no new runtime law. A tilted hinge/nonzero rest rotation would require a separately scoped rest-relative controller change; do not silently export that change while retaining direct Euler assignments.

The processing lattice must be fitted **inside** the new skull rather than dictate an external chin shelf. Root should include the local `processing` subtree in the head fit scope: retain the named processing function/inspection anchor and Advanced-only eligibility, but rebuild/reseat rails and inner support endpoints. It is not permissible to relabel these powered/processing parts as passive bronze and expose them in Maker. The present head module's `OWNERS` excludes `processing`; retaining that exclusion unchanged would leave the actual chin-stick outside the reconstruction.

## Contact and socket updates

`bill-contact` is parented to `upper-bill`; `anchor-beak` to `head`. Both currently coincide at **(0, −.797985792, 1.578891516)**. After forming the new bill, derive the actual evaluated leading upper-bill surface point (minimum native Y), then seat both markers there using each parent's inverse transform. Keep contact geometry under named `upper-bill`, including its finite cutting/hook surface. A jaw-only pivot relocation does **not** move these upper-bill markers.

`createEraMotion` uses actual exported **upper-bill triangles** at the selected rail centre plane, and derives approach reach from those triangles; it does not rely on a stale fixed beak offset (`src/scene/era-motion.js:77–126`). `presence-exhibit.js:220–224,351` still uses the named markers for inspection and tip reporting. Thus update markers **and** actual bill surface, without pretending marker placement alone repairs contact.

If procedural Maker hardware is included, derive a finite jaw control attachment on the new jaw. `createEraMechanisms` consumes `body.mechanismLayoutV1.makerControlOffsets.jaw` in glTF jaw-local coordinates, with fallback **[−.18, −.06, .20]** (`src/scene/era-mechanisms.js:161–170`). Exact Form02 has only historical `priorV21_mechanismLayoutV1`, not an active `mechanismLayoutV1`; that historical JSON does not drive the current socket. Root must update a complete valid active layout or separately fit the runtime fallback; no socket-fit claim follows from the pivot change alone. `anchor-mind` remains a `processing` child and should follow the fitted processor location.
