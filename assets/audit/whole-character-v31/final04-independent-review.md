In profile, the lower jaw does not yet read as a substantial curved mandible meeting the upper bill; the open pose still looks like a thin appendage under a mostly fixed face. Preserve the distinction between the owner target’s nearly closed neutral mouth and July’s clearly open jaw.
- **Still wrong:** the orbital assembly remains dominated by a large circular bearing/ring and bars. The owner image integrates a bright optic into diagonal brow and cheek plating; the current grayscale render cannot establish material/color, but the circular hardware silhouette is plainly generic.
- **Still wrong:** the crown segmentation is an improvement, but broad smooth plates still form a helmet-like hood. The reference has a more swept-back, overlapping cranial flow around the eye. The neck now connects more coherently, though the head-to-throat region remains smooth and mask-like.
- **Disposition:** retain the contour/segmentation changes as a local study direction, not as a likeness result. The bill-jaw profile and orbital facade still need substantial shape work; no owner acceptance or finished-head claim follows from these views.
