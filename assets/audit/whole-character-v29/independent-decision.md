# V29 independent decision

V29 Fit01 is a bounded regional construction proposal, not an accepted character. In the reviewed reference-angle and neck views, the external head, neck and breast silhouette remains effectively Form02. The folded wing retains compact, continuous canopy coverage.

The breast-hinge study is a useful local mechanical fit: fixed clevises and moving annular seats address the existing shaft interface without moving its pivot. The wing-interface study also appears worth retaining locally; it preserves the folded canopy while shaping a recessed receiving interface. Their receipts and sampled screens do not establish full-motion clearance, engineering validity or likeness.

Exclude both V29 neck-receiver studies. Neck02 removes the sampled neck-to-body/breast crossings but leaves a dark, unframed notch between the throat and chest; it reads as missing coverage and makes the neck appear detached. Its returns cross fixed side, front and backing surfaces and shoulder links. Neither is a usable neck solution.

The remaining high-level likeness gaps are the broad smooth chest, generic head/face, sparse-looking legs and weak shoulder-to-torso connection. The adjacent cervical rigid-owner seams also remain unresolved; a finite articulated joint treatment must restore coverage without those crossings. No appearance, continuous-clearance or owner-acceptance claim is made.
