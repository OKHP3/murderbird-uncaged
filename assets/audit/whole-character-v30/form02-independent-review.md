# V30 Form02 independent visual review

**Disposition: HOLD.** Compared the actual Form02 [three-quarter](attempt-form02/after-reference-angle.png), [front](attempt-form02/after-front.png), [side](attempt-form02/after-side.png), [rear](attempt-form02/after-rear.png) and [neck](attempt-form02/after-neck.png) renders with the matched V29 views and the owner reference. This is a visual assessment of the neutral study, not owner acceptance or an engineering verdict.

Form02 makes a useful body-shape change: the front chest now tapers more clearly toward the lower frame, and the side/rear no longer read as the same round segmented barrel visible in V29. The new outer shell gives the chest a more deliberate armor silhouette. That is a real directional improvement, but it remains a few large, smooth shield surfaces; the rear is still a broad blank teardrop and the shoulder-to-breast transition lacks the reference's layered, mechanically dense connection.

The face is not yet convincing. The bill's outside contour is more compact, but its blunt, nearly straight lower edge and straight mandible leave the face reading like a smooth beak mask. The optic remains a large, plain circular insert, without the nested, angled machinery that gives the owner image its expression. The crown and cheek parts frame the eye but do not yet make a coherent, swept facial assembly.

The leg chain remains visually light beneath the torso: exposed narrow members, prominent round joints and a long open foot/toe assembly do not carry the reference's heavy, articulated stance. The folded wings also read as repeated scallops hanging beside the torso, rather than a compact, integrated shoulder shield.

The three-quarter body taper is worth retaining as a provisional direction. The blunt bill/mandible and unintegrated optic, plain rear/shoulder mass, and sparse lower limbs still prevent a material likeness correction. Gray neutral renders expose shape only; they do not demonstrate the final appearance. All three era appearances remain unaccepted, and source visibility does not establish complete era hardware.
