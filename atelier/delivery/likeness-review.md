# First Choice • Advanced likeness review

**Show the frozen candidate privately. Hold publication and a finished-likeness claim.** The hooked bill now projects in front of the breast and the swept crown balances the folded wing. In the matched native portrait, bill overhang increased from about 18 to 80 pixels in the final head-position pass. The largest remaining differences are the bright, fully exposed optic, simpler armor courses and narrower stance.

## Visual comparison

- **Head and neck.** Retained04 has a gap between visor and bill root. The candidate’s brow resolves into a deep hook, its cheek returns into the bill root and its jaw remains open. Advancing and enlarging the head places the bill clearly beyond the breast, while the curved upper cervical linkage remains visible. September’s projected head proportions are now close. The candidate’s eye still sits farther aft within its own head silhouette. July wraps a shaded, irregular brow over the upper optic. Here the complete circular rim and a broad, smooth gold cap soften the expression.
- **Mantle and rear.** The rounded near-wing cap of retained04 has become a compact set of sheets tapering behind the thigh. September has finer, denser overlapping courses that turn around the shoulder. The candidate has a small contrasting left fitting, but a viewer cannot read two distinct periods of shoulder repair from it.
- **Finish and construction history.** Plate boundaries, dark joints, wear and grain are visible. The candidate carries light warm brass over a broad part of the brow, throat and breast, plus a pale gray-green near wing. September and July carry darker metal between warm worn edges and fasteners. The native surfaces remain smoother and cleaner overall. Lighting differs between the native and illustrated views.
- **Legs and feet.** The candidate exposes the near hinge and lower-leg linkage and gives the talons distinct contact silhouettes. September has a much wider projected stance, heavier articulated ankle and digit covers, and more varied silhouettes down the leg. The candidate’s rectangular lower struts and small repair markers convey less of that accumulated history.
