# Three-era motion and sound recordings

These recordings come from the November 24 three-era study on Apple M4 Max. The recordings capture the onscreen mechanism or procedural audio. The file identities, frame counts, dimensions and audio sample counts are in the [capture manifest](manifest.json). The [standalone viewer](../three-eras-offline.html) lets you operate the same edition.

| What to examine | Recording |
| --- | --- |
| External operation and a bounded gear cycle | [Maker controls and Mechanic winding](maker-mechanic.webm). The [uncut Maker idle trial](maker-uncut-idle.webm) includes a minute without operator input. |
| Advanced responses to interaction | [Encounter controls](advanced-encounter.webm). Two fixed-camera recordings show the approach to the rail from [the front](advanced-front-patrol-reach.webm) and [the left](advanced-left-patrol-reach.webm). |
| Reversible access to components | Open, inspect and reassemble the [Maker bill](maker-regional-return.webm), [Mechanic left leg](mechanic-regional-return.webm) or [Advanced optic](advanced-regional-return.webm). The [panel comparison](selected-mechanisms.jpg) shows the head, breast and service selections across all three eras. |
| Exterior continuity | Compare the [orbit stills](orbit-views.jpg), then follow the full turns for [Maker](maker-360.webm), [Mechanic](mechanic-360.webm) and [Advanced](advanced-360.webm). Each orbit records 72 five-degree steps. |
| Self-directed motion | The [first](advanced-watch-1.mp4) and [second](advanced-watch-2.mp4) enclosure trials each cover two minutes without further visitor input. Different random seeds produced distinct action orderings and timing in these two trials. |
| Sound | Compare stereo PCM captures for the [Maker lever](foley-maker.wav), [Mechanic winding](foley-mechanic.wav), [Advanced First Choice](foley-advanced.wav), [patrol](foley-patrol.wav) and [visitor approach](foley-approach.wav). The browser generated these signals. Human listening and first-time visitor trials remain open. |

The WebM and MP4 recordings are silent. The WAV files contain the separate foley captures at 48 kHz. For runtime details and the limits of the artistic review, see the [study guide](../README.md). Creative rights remain with Jamie Hill / OverKill Hill P³ under the [content notice](../NOTICE.md).
