## Current priority and next deliverable

4. After owner review establishes the desired visual direction, a return to mechanics and fitment may be proposed as a separate phase. That phase requires its own explicit owner authorization and bounded plan. Approval of a visual checkpoint is not authorization to resume engineering work.

## Visual construction rules

No manufacturing, tolerance, finite-support, manifold, printability or parts-accounting gate applies. Simplify or fake invisible structures. Intersections are permitted wherever they produce no visible Z-fighting or distracting artifacts in reviewed views and motion. Prefer clean Sub-D cages for hero forms; use normal/height/displacement maps for non-silhouette detail. Choose visual quality over engineering purity and state the tradeoff.

Every implementation checkpoint supplies eight-angle turntable views, same-camera canon comparisons, exhibit-lit hero views and, when its milestone includes animation, idle/walk evidence. Silhouette, proportions and layering govern the image review. After two consecutive iterations without visible improvement, stop and report.

## Production boundaries

Use the released V37 assembly and useful retained V38 work as the starting material, preserving both. Keep MurderBird mechanical, avian and flightless, with folded ornamental shield-wings (intimidation, weapon, shield — never for lift), the restricted anatomical left shoulder and compact rear contour. Do not introduce biological tissue, dinosaur anatomy, or flight surfaces. Preserve useful era, movement and inspection controls; inspection should remain visually purposeful, without turning the assignment into engineering documentation.

The July selection controls head identity only; candidate 03 controls the common body direction; the selected era images control their respective finish; the First Choice video informs surface continuity and screen presence. Preserve their documented scopes and actual source binaries. Newly inferred details remain proposals.

Choose an editable, browser-reliable surface pipeline that visibly supplies regional detail. Geometry, texture maps, procedural detail and lighting are means to the CG result. Do not pursue a pipeline merely to accumulate technical artifacts. Preserve versioned native sources and historical assets.

The visual gate is a recognizable MurderBird whose color, surface character and overall appearance closely match the pinned reference set across eras, cameras and lighting, subject to owner artistic review. Automated checks, worker agreement and deployment do not establish that acceptance. No engineering certification is required or claimed. Later mechanics/fitment is a separate follow-on priority; do not describe it as completed by this visual stage.
