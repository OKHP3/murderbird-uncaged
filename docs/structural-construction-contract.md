## Mechanical interpretation

The load frame serves as bones; bearings and hinges define joints; external operation, wound transmission, or Advanced actuators provide motion; rods, restraints, and conduits connect systems; rigid armor and joint guards form the exterior. There is no biological tissue, soft organic body, or skeletal skinning. Plates and bearing housings are rigid children of their local assembly. Runtime connecting links update from actual attachment transforms. Flexible/variable-span routes are separate reconstructions, not deformed armor.

Coordinates below are **measured authored model coordinates in metres**, in Blender axes: X anatomical left, Z up, −Y forward. Browser conversion is `(X,Z,−Y)`. They are design choices, not dimensions recovered from perspective art. The convention remains approximately two metres at the crown; the exact bounding height depends on posture and excludes the Maker support. The runtime floor solve places the soles about 2 mm above the exhibit floor.

## Boundaries of the editable source

The `.blend` preserves separate editable plates, fasteners, rigid groups, landmarks, neutral materials, and one attention export-proof clip. The `.glb` batches those meshes by moving assembly/material. The procedural Maker support/control rack, Mechanic spring/cam/transmission, Advanced distribution/actuators and short tail remain editable runtime construction in `src/scene/era-mechanisms.js`; they are **not baked into the Blender file**. Continuous encounter animation is procedural, not an authored animation library. Neither the model nor validation certifies a real machine, physical simulation, collision-free unsampled poses, or final likeness.

## Remaining artistic decisions

The independent reference review identifies a still narrow/faceted bill, boxy crown, tall/straight stacked neck and repetitive breast plate rhythm. Owner judgment is required on the revised head–neck–breast relationship, bill depth/curvature, swept crown silhouette, compact guard size and shoulder placement. Final armor overlap rhythm, surface materials/wear, detailed unseen rear/underside construction, and the repair's precise appearance remain open. The early review gate is explicit and unresolved until the owner responds; technical checks cannot close it.
