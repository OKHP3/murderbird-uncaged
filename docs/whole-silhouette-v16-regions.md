## Inheritance and remaining reconstructed hardware

Maker remains externally operated and anchored. Mechanic retains a limited wound drive, slow steps and segmented turns. Advanced retains inherited structure and repairs with selective power, actuators, sensing and processing. No material presets, wear maps or powered parts were added in this proportion pass. The original story and its finite-power language remain unchanged; the abundant-power exhibit premise remains separately documented.

The source review at `assets/audit/whole-silhouette-v16/runtime-attachment-review.md` distinguishes model-derived endpoints from fixed local recipes. Feet, leg link lengths, hip/knee actuator ends, wing ends, power-core source and bill contact derive from the loaded model. Maker control horns, Advanced cervical anchors, Mechanic crank sources, support width and some conduit routes still have authored local offsets. Passing motion checks does not validate those offsets against the new armor.

Historical authoring curves are preserved and refitted in the editable scene, explicitly tagged and hidden from rendering. They are not presented as working cables or biological anatomy. Original references, older native files and failed proposals remain intact. Fine surface treatment, material history, whole-body collision freedom and owner likeness acceptance remain outstanding.
