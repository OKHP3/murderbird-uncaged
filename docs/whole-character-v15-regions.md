## Inherited regions and current visual limits

Neck, wings/mantle, rear assembly, and original foot/talon/contact geometry were not redesigned here. They remain inherited from V14 and have not thereby received likeness acceptance. The leg proposal adds only the named rigid trusses and toe-bearing flanges on those existing owners; it does not alter the feet or claws themselves.

The independent image review in `assets/audit/whole-character-v15/attempt-03/independent-visual-review.md` remains relevant to attempt04's unchanged regional silhouettes. It found the head outline closer but still too smooth and optically blank; the breast coverage fuller but its broad, regular courses unlike the references' dense small feather-like plates; and the whole figure longer-necked, more tapered, and simpler in torso and leg silhouette than the compact, rounded, densely layered references. Attempt04 does not resolve those findings by composition alone.

This is an editable local proposal, not finished textures, a validated mechanism, continuous-clearance proof, physical simulation, or owner-accepted likeness. The diagnostic GLB and fresh 21-pose packet are bound in the companion checkpoint; neither constitutes artistic or full-clearance acceptance. No publication is asserted here.
