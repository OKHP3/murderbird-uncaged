# V18 lower neck reconstruction — two held candidates

**Neither candidate clears the acceptance gates.** Attempt01 is the better visual fallback because it removes the suspended anterior cup and retains a closed-looking breast junction. Attempt02 slopes the breast opening but exposes a broad dark gap and adds failed frame/upper-neck fits. Both editable natives, executed sources, matching views and strict 21-pose evidence are preserved. The live regional module is restored byte-for-byte to attempt01's frozen source; this is a held fallback, not an integration or default-model approval. No third candidate was made.

## Why attempt02 leaves a visible gap

[Read-only evaluated bounds](../assets/audit/whole-character-v18/attempt-neck-reconstruction02/readonly-junction-bounds.json) explain the regression:

| Breast front lip, absolute X≈0.150 | maximum 1.2799395 under the same filter | Y −0.3737..−0.3633 |

The guard endpoints are close to the lip vertically but stop tens of millimetres behind it and cover a much narrower span. Dropping/receding the opening without continuing a shaped shoulder/underlap into that cavity exposes a dark band rather than a closed neck-to-breast surface. The existing backing was rebuilt only down to approximately Z 1.29 m; it was not extended to a deeper cavity seat. This is an authored geometric shortcoming, not an unavoidable gap implied by the character or a validated service aperture.

A coherently flared finite underlap continuing toward Z≈1.18 m behind the lip is a plausible next construction, but has not been made or tested. Merely lengthening these endpoints would not settle the depth/width mismatch or the moving third/fourth lame fit. At center X=0 the actual02 shell front lies at Y −0.45535 at Z1.18, −0.45454 at Z1.22 and −0.43297 at Z1.26; a new underlap would need to fit the real cavity rather than repeat the collar or cut arbitrary holes. Current strike/body crossings do not prove such a reconstruction impossible. Containment, actual supports and all sampled relative motions would need review before claiming success.
