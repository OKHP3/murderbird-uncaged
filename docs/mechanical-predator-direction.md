# Owner direction — strength, machinery and predatory speed

**2026-09-27 · active creative instruction · model acceptance remains pending.**

The owner clarified the intended combination after asking for the first model's proportions to be reworked:

> Imagine if it was a combination of T2 terminator without the skin, and it was the super battle droid from Clone Wars mix with the Velociraptor from Jurassic Park, if all those were together and then it was any birdlike form rather than a dinosaur type form, that kind of beef, that kind of strength you would have from those bots with the speed, the ferocity of the Velociraptor

The owner specifically identified the **B2-series super battle droid**. This is current owner direction, not a recovered historical prompt or approval of a completed model.

## Translation into MurderBird

| Cue | Design interpretation | Concrete local revision |
|---|---|---|
| Exposed Terminator machinery | Visible load paths, joint housings, actuator bodies and piston rods that explain the strength under the shell. | Thicker thigh and shin mechanisms, thoracic yoke, pelvic braces and cervical actuators. Exact construction is proposed. |
| B2 mass and strength | A broad shoulder frame and deep trunk that look capable of delivering and absorbing force. | Wider, deeper torso and compact mantles; larger knee/ankle housings; less head-to-body dominance. |
| Jurassic Park raptor speed and ferocity | Poised, economical attention followed by fast, decisive movement; legs visibly ready to drive the body. | Forward-set head and compact neck, bent supported legs and broad planted toes. Production locomotion and claw strike are still outstanding. |
| Birdlike form | MurderBird's hooked bill, circular optic, swept crown, folded mantles and avian feet remain recognizable. | Recessed/smaller optic housing and curved bill profile; no added human arms, dinosaur muzzle or balancing tail. |
| Existing MurderBird material history | Dense overlapping darkened bronze, patina in recesses and purposeful repair hardware. | Closed lapped armor replaces the earlier bright outlined cell pattern; visible mechanism windows stay at joints. Material finish is still a study. |

The interpretation should read as a heavy mechanical bird capable of sudden speed. Extra bulk must not produce a waddling posture, human chest/waist, or a static armored barrel. The named characters explain traits; their names, logos, weapons, copied meshes and story identities are not added to the exhibit.

## Source context and limits

The [official Star Wars Databank entry](https://www.starwars.com/databank/super-battle-droid), retrieved 2026-09-27, identifies the B2 and describes its increased bulk and strength. It supports identification of the owner's reference; its weapons and independent operation are not MurderBird requirements.

The [INART endoskeleton product reference](https://global.inart.studio/products/terminator-endoskeleton-1-6-figure), retrieved 2026-09-27, provides supplementary exposed-machine images. It depicts a collectible based on *The Terminator* and is not treated as an exact T2 production reference or a usable mesh source. No purchase or asset download occurred. The raptor cue is taken directly from the owner's stated speed and ferocity; no scientific claim about real Velociraptor anatomy is made.

Candidate 03 and the owner-preferred July head remain appearance references within their recorded scope in [creative authority](creative-authority.md). This new instruction controls the emphasis on strength and speed. It does not silently amend the story, certify unseen engineering, or approve publication.
