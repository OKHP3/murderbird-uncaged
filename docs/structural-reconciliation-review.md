## Editable source, runtime, and preservation

Authoring is `scripts/build-uncaged-structure-v1.py`. It hash-checks the original `.blend`, then writes the new [editable Blender file](../assets/models/uncaged-structure-v1/murderbird-structure-v1.blend) and [runtime GLB](../assets/models/uncaged-structure-v1/murderbird-structure-v1.glb). The source preserves 1,276 separate mesh objects and 39 empties; the GLB batches to 23 meshes / 62 nodes / 63,468 indexed triangles. Neither uses skinning. One proof clip is exported; continuous behavior is procedural.

The runtime-only external controls, drives, actuators and short tail remain editable in `src/scene/era-mechanisms.js`; they are not baked into the Blender file. The rigid leg/toe geometry is retained. The original study, mass, shield, presence and three-era artifacts, selected references and original story snapshot were preserved. Selected LFS sources were hydrated only from hash-verified identical preserved media. No source archives or review materials were placed in `public/`.

The regional contract documents controlling reference, exterior envelope, frame and joint relationship, pivots, movement/source by era, rigid/flexible choices, clearance, inspection and confirmed/reconstructed status. It deliberately assigns no exact dimensions to perspective artwork. July controls only the head; candidate 03 and the scoped era images control their specified features. The body remains a flightless bird with compact shielding wings and a short rear.

## Practical limits and next decision

These checks prove sampled kinematic continuity, contact geometry and interface behavior. They do not prove full triangle collision freedom, every plate's clearance throughout continuous motion, centre of gravity, friction, force transfer, a physically closed linkage, or engineering feasibility. The short sliding links and exact actuator topology remain illustrative reconstructions. The slow-frame integrator caps elapsed simulation at 100 ms; long stalls can lose simulation time without producing a large single foot jump. Human screen-reader review and physical Safari/Firefox/phone coverage remain unverified.

The next artistic pass should strengthen skull/cheek mass and crown sweep, recover a deeper convex bill, shorten/broaden the neck bridge while retaining clearance, and improve the lower-breast/pelvis transition. The owner must choose the direction at the early gate before any finished exterior treatment. This is a concrete editable checkpoint for that decision, not a claim that the full assignment's recognizable-character exit condition has been reached.
