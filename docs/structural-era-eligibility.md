This matrix reconciles the preserved presence study with the versioned structural derivative and runtime mechanisms. The [construction contract](structural-construction-contract.md) owns region dimensions and visual review; this document assigns era eligibility. ## Inspection and attachment rules

The base asset is a set of rigid assemblies; the exhibit adds procedural mechanisms. Do not merge all era drives into the shared body or treat `visible` toggles as a substitute for correct ownership/parenting.

- A body-fixed or limb-fixed component follows its actual parent assembly. Mechanic’s spring/gearbox follows the body; its cam-to-knee link endpoints update from the transformed knee. The variable-span bars are an explicit proposed sliding linkage, not powered cylinders.
- Builder’s core and distribution are separate assemblies. At the normal pose, the conduit joins their sockets. When inspection separates the core, the continuous cable hides and two short socket stubs remain. Leg routes start at distribution. Shoulder/elbow actuator and supply links likewise disconnect into short stubs when those joints separate. Reassembly restores the normal connections without carrying a prior offset.
- Maker rods terminate at explicit joint horns and continue to follow those attachment transforms. The support, levers, horns and lines are Maker-only; the root remains anchored while a control moves an individual joint.
- Opening a cover may expose the corresponding assembly, but must not drag internal load frame members with the cover or leave them floating. In particular, the passive thorax stays with the body while the breast cover opens; the skull stays attached to the upper cervical pivot while its roof lifts. Test the actual cover and separation poses, not just the default assembly.

The headless Three scene check verifies era-group eligibility, five Maker targets/horns, Mechanic sliding-link lengths, Builder cervical/wing/power connector modes, exploded socket stubs, and reconnection after restoring rest transforms. It does not render or establish that rods visually meet hidden attachment faces. Local GLB hierarchy/identity checks are recorded separately. The owner’s likeness, visual fit, unsampled clearance, real stability, and force behavior remain unverified; browser and visual review must complete before any user-facing visual claim.

## Change rule

When extending the model, classify each new mesh or link as **passive shared structure**, **Maker external control**, **Mechanic single-spring transmission**, **Advanced supply/distribution/actuation**, or **unresolved**. Record the actual attachment node and inspection behavior with that classification. If a component looks powered (piston, cylinder, cable take-off, bus, optic), either make it era-specific or state and implement why it is passive. Keep the Blender source editable, GLB rigid assemblies grouped by moving joint, and all creative rights/status language consistent with [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md) and [project authority](creative-authority.md).
