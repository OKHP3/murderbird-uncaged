## Material and production decisions

V36 is a rigid geometry and regional material study, with eight exported material definitions and no new image-texture/UV finishing pass. Temporary Cycles neutral overrides only expose form; they are not intended exhibit materials or an exportable procedural shader pipeline. Existing materials and history remain preserved. Three era-specific finished exteriors, local wear, manufacturing marks, protected advanced additions and faithful aged repairs are still undelivered.

Regeneration is write-once into a new attempt directory with pinned input hashes. Each attempt retains its native source, invoked region scripts, receipts and evidence. Manual or historical sources are not overwritten. The failed integrated Form01 native/GLB remains immutable even if a later regional attempt succeeds.

See the [checkpoint](whole-character-v36-checkpoint.md) for exact candidate hashes, before/after views, independent failed fit evidence, runtime checks and remaining artistic decisions.
