## Correction to the working method

The loop ran too long without a whole-bird checkpoint. Recent orbital and cervical variants consumed substantial effort while remaining excluded from the selected model. Small-model workers are useful for bounded preservation, measurements and regression checks; repeated local fitting attempts have not resolved the unified character design.

The next production cycle should have one supervisor-owned whole-body candidate, beginning with the neck-to-breast and shoulder relationship. Require matched full-body views before local detail checks, then a bounded movement review. Retain a regional change only when it improves the common silhouette and preserves motion. Do not commission another sequence of optic cutbacks, polish materials, or publish while the principal likeness findings remain unresolved. Repair chronology and historical motion-source scope remain documented owner decisions for a concrete later review packet.

This checkpoint preserves work and makes the remaining gap visible; it is not completion of the correction mandate.
