# Exterior regional map — version 1 proposal

This map assigns reference authority and rigid ownership for the modular exterior proposal. It separates visible source evidence from reconstructed geometry. The references are illustrations, not engineering drawings. Exact thicknesses, hidden surfaces, fasteners, and fabrication remain proposed. Source-level placement checks and motion checks have distinct scope; neither establishes likeness approval. The rejected intermediate geometry is preserved under `assets/audit/exterior-v1/provisional/`; current top-level renders and dated receipts refer to the frozen review model.

## Evidence and review status

The source modules tag authored pieces with `region`, `surfaceRole`, `inherited`, and `exteriorEras`. The explicit era tag controls runtime eligibility. Joined export `surfaceRole` values summarize mixed material roles, and the generic `inherited` flag remains incorrect on some Advanced-only additions in this frozen version. Use the table above and detailed inventory/material primitives for chronology and role; these are proposed construction, not recovered history. Current neutral renders show the deeper bill hook, exposed paired leg rails, shorter fitted guards, staggered breast seams, and compact layered shoulder mantles. Historical iteration A and rejected intermediate diagnostics remain separately identified; they are not the current runtime.

The [stage record](exterior-stage-record.md) identifies the exact GLB and current source, asset, motion and browser receipts. Source placement checks cover 56 outer wing overlays and retirement of the old solid thigh tubes. The three-millimetre plate walls, seven-millimetre mantle row offsets and four-millimetre forewing layer steps are authored values, not dimensions inferred from perspective art. Sampled visibility and motion checks are bounded evidence, not continuous collision or physical-load certification.

Artistic acceptance remains pending. Specific review points are the crown/bill identity, broad folded-wing contour around the shoulder and elbow, breast taper and panel rhythm, compact foot treatment, proposed repair landmarks, and the amount of local wear. The references do not establish exact hidden surfaces, alloy chemistry, repair topology or historical mechanics. The original story and source snapshots remain unchanged.
