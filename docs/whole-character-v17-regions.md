# Whole-character V17 regional proposal

V17 develops the breast exterior and head construction on the V16 rest rig. The V16 regional contract remains the authority for unchanged regions, pose ownership, and existing era behavior; see [the V16 region contract](whole-silhouette-v16-regions.md). Attempt02 is a held local proposal, not an approved likeness, finished model, or published change. Its native SHA-256 is `7d371907b279eb3d625e67164d84a78b2901a5cb6a79917def07f48ff8792d7b`.

## Breast shell and exterior

The controlling whole-bird sources are the owner target, Candidate03, and the selected Maker and Mechanic images listed in the attempt02 receipt. They guide visible form and construction language; their differing perspective does not provide recoverable dimensions. The July image is head-only and does not govern body or breast proportions.

Attempt02 reshapes the breastplate-owned `Breast inner access shell` and bakes two subdivision levels into its editable rigid mesh. The shell's upper shoulder is receded by at most 20 mm, tapered laterally, and blended over 245 mm; it is not unchanged. Against that evaluated shell, the candidate builds eight courses of 78 tapered directional plates, smaller toward the flanks, with two center-keel returns and 78 captive fixing heads (81 newly created breast meshes total). The authored wall thickness is 6 mm and course stand-off ranges from 3.5 to 6 mm. These are reconstructed proposal dimensions, not measurements from perspective illustrations.

The shell, plates, returns, and fixings belong to the existing `breastplate` assembly and move with its opening transform. The 78 plates are passive and tagged for Maker, Mechanic, and Advanced (`builder`) visibility. No new pivot, powered function, or flexible metal is introduced. The breast opening pivot, internal frame, material definitions, and all 52 pivots and parent/world matrices are retained. References and exact geometry counts are recorded in [the attempt02 receipt](../assets/audit/whole-character-v17/attempt-02/receipt.json).

## Head construction

Attempt02 changes the two existing `Forged orbital mounting plate` meshes into upper/rear arc panels and adds 12 passive pieces: two retaining races, six support bridges, and four round attachment forms. These are head-owned and tagged for Maker, Mechanic, and Advanced visibility. The existing recessed bearings, housings, optics, fixings, bill, jaw, and pivots retain their owners; the two Advanced-only optic tags remain unchanged. The round forms have unknown function and are not classified as sensors. Intentional race/arc mating in the construction recipe is not a fit or clearance proof.

## Strict screen and held status

The read-only strict recheck evaluated four hash-bound runtime pose samples: inspection closed, Maker neck control, Advanced contact, and inspection at 25% open. It found strict triangle crossings in every sampled pose. The new races/forms intersect the moving crown laminae and root pins. The reshaped access shell crosses passive body ribs, and first-course breast plates cross those ribs and cervical flank/throat laminae. This is a sampled surface-intersection screen, not a continuous sweep or penetration-depth measurement; its exact pair records are in [strict-crossings.json](../assets/audit/whole-character-v17/attempt-02/strict-regional-followup/strict-crossings.json).

**Attempt02 remains held.** Its successful kinematic, inspection, power-move, claw, and pose-render checks do not override these crossings or establish artistic acceptance. Do not promote the model by default.

## Inherited regions and remaining review

All other regional construction and runtime contracts remain as documented in V16. V17 does not change the 52 pivots, wings, legs, feet, pelvis, back, or runtime mechanisms. The lower-neck/throat-to-breast junction still needs coherent visual review; crown and optic clearance remain open; fixed-offset procedural hardware still needs fit review; and the inherited leg/foot construction has not received V17 review. Future work must resolve the sampled breast/rib/neck and orbital/crown crossings while preserving the intended assembly ownership and era visibility. No continuous clearance, physical strength, browser appearance, owner acceptance, or publication result is claimed.
