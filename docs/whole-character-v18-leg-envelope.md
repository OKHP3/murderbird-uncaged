# Whole-character V18 lower-leg construction study

Attempt `leg-envelope06` is a bounded passive-geometry proposal built from the pinned V17 attempt-02 native (`7d371907b279eb3d625e67164d84a78b2901a5cb6a79917def07f48ff8792d7b`). The evaluated owner targets are the MurderBird reference supplied for this review and the selected Maker/Mechanic images at `assets/img/library/murderbird-unified-maker-clean-candidate-2026-09-06.png` and `assets/img/library/murderbird-unified-mechanic-candidate-2026-09-06.png`. The proposal gives the lower leg more formed section while retaining open service access; it is not an accepted likeness or validated engineering model.

The module is `scripts/regions/whole-character-v18-leg-envelope.py` (SHA-256 `d9e921a6df20ce2d5283a70b2b1b9d2e5a55f93e7d508683f6e58cfee59bbaa6`). It adds one passive split shin web and one ankle-to-foot-root yoke on each side. The new webs belong to `left-shin` and `right-shin`; the yokes belong to the corresponding foot owners. They have Maker, Mechanic and Builder eligibility, use inherited frame/bearing materials, and add no powered parts. The web ends short of the knee and ankle pivots. The yoke ends are sampled from each actual foot bearing core and the two retained metatarsal rails.

The explicit replacement allowlist is `Left metatarsus open passive truss` and `Right metatarsus open passive truss`. Each keeps its object name, foot parent, object transform and rail anchors; only its mesh data is replaced by a tapered C-channel with split dorsal shoulders and large lateral service windows. The former ladder-like diagonal is no longer the visible load surface. Both original metatarsal rails, the curved instep guards, the rear hallux links, toe/claw geometry, bearings and pivot transforms remain. The channel is fitted inside the source truss's measured projected span, with a proximal ankle-end relief in attempt 06. The receipt field `sourceProjectedSpanM` is misnamed: its value (`0.78`) is a dimensionless fraction of the foot-to-toe pivot span, not meters. The image comparison is in [the attempt-06 audit folder](../assets/audit/whole-character-v18/attempt-leg-envelope06/).

The V18 composer records two changed source meshes, four added meshes and none removed. It confirms all 52 pivots, 462 historical guides, 711 unchanged source meshes, unchanged material definitions and save/reopen identity. The corrected V06 regional return explicitly lists the two modified trusses. V05's frozen intermediate module return mistakenly said `existingMeshesModified: []`; its composer receipt still recorded the two changed names. That historical receipt remains untouched and is superseded for this scope by the accurate V06 receipt.

The bounded clearance screen replays 21 pinned runtime pose matrices and six claw-action stages. Its inputs are the V17 GLB (`52980d9578d985737dfe7595515be494d9b1bcfa2dc17d960f18ac6f6fe6049e`), the 21-pose packet (`1964918661da857878376ff95457f3b8e0bc73479bd83225a8419fb1e2a627f9`), and a separately preserved six-claw-stage packet generated from that same GLB. Maximum replay matrix error is `2.39e-7`; the native-versus-packet rest error is `1.49e-8`. These are deterministic discrete kinematic samples, not browser pose captures.

Across those 27 samples, the scope-limited screen found **zero strict different-owner crossings** between the altered/new lower-leg surfaces and same-side thigh, shin, foot or toe-owner meshes, including **zero toe-owner BVH candidates** in the six claw stages. The V05 intersections at the ankle are absent after the single V06 end relief. The evaluated surfaces were tested with the frozen strict noncoplanar `proper_crossing_receipt` kernel (`assets/audit/cervical-construction-study-v1/attempt-07/actual-runtime-joint-clearance-v1/executed-review.py`, SHA-256 `6d840608cf43b20beccaa8a7f1cab274d000485996ea2b1a6e1460921092b356`).

The same screen reports 32 same-owner strict crossing identities recurring across all 27 samples: each channel crosses the two retained rail surfaces, its curved instep guard and rear hallux link; each yoke crosses its bearing core, instep guard and two rails; each shin web crosses six existing sheath fixings and two bearing races. These contacts are not automatically accepted as fits. The yoke-to-bearing/rail intersections are the proposed fixed attachment areas; channel/rail, channel/instep/hallux and web/fixing/race seating still need specific fit review. No full containment, continuous swept-volume, actuator-guide or load analysis was performed.

The leg closeups and matched whole-body views use the same neutral native Workbench render style. At exhibit scale the lower-leg structures read more substantial and the new metatarsal channel replaces the repeated open zigzag. The hip/knee forms and overall character likeness remain open. No runtime, application, export, publication or commit changes were made.
