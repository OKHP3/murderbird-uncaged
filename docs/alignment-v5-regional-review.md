## What the correction loop changed

The original sleeve-like limb surfaces became shaped, overlapping thigh/shin plates and curved instep guards. The first combined candidate still had four distracting instep ridges and swollen claw roots. Guard study06 replaces those ridges with one broad shallow crown. Talon study03 flattens and broadens the six distal sheaths while keeping their owners and terminal contact rings. The measured centerline error is at most approximately 0.11 micrometres; it is not a claim of zero floating-point error. For these six talon sheaths, the original c8 lateral and ventral envelopes are not exceeded.

The independent comparison now distinguishes that improvement from the unchanged round proximal links and knuckles. The foot-to-claw assembly still needs a more coherent transition, and side-view guard coverage remains less convincing than the front and three-quarter views. The head, neck, body, mantle and exposed joint construction remain subject to their separate visual review. No texture or wear pass conceals these issues.

The [gallery packet](../assets/audit/alignment-v5-regional/review-packet-candidate04-final.json) binds six base/current views, a separate candidate02/current regional comparison, three native era configurations, source art and motion evidence. Camera positions, targets, orthographic scales, renderer identities and image hashes are checked against the render receipts.
