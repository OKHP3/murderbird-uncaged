## Surface and export contract

This is a geometry correction, not the finished material stage. Existing material definitions are retained; the derivative uses the existing rigid-mesh/glTF material path. No image textures, UV production pipeline, normal maps or new shader dependency are introduced by this pass. Authoring-space reform changes the rest shape of rigid parts; it does not deform metal during animation.

Native sources are versioned Blender files, and exports preserve distinct named mesh nodes, rigid owners, extras and material assignments. The isolated viewer can show neutral construction shading or the exported materials and era source visibility. The complete local exhibit adds its retained external controls, transmission and Advanced equipment. Those two views have different scopes and must not be described as equivalent complete assemblies.

New ordinary passive pieces inherit three-era visibility. Processing, sensing and Advanced actuation remain Advanced-only; Maker is anchored and externally operated; Mechanic keeps its limited stepped sequence. No story text or provenance label is rewritten to make the reconstruction seem historically recovered.

## What remains artistic work

The overall bird, regional armor language and all three finished era exteriors remain unaccepted. The bulbous breast envelope and broad plate rhythm, round optic emphasis, crown/neck transitions, leg density and detailed repair landmarks still need judgment against the selected references in multiple poses and lights. Neutral and exhibit lighting demonstrate the same construction; a prettier light is not a likeness correction.
