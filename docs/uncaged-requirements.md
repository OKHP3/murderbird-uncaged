# MurderBird: Uncaged — working requirements

This is a local product requirements record for the prototype and its next acceptance gates. It consolidates the supplied owner mandate with current repository rules and the lead-selected creative direction in [creative authority](creative-authority.md) and [unified direction](murderbird-unified-direction.md). It does not amend story canon, approve final art, or authorize publication.

**Current gate:** Three distinct era controllers are implemented locally over the preserved Stage Two body: externally operated Maker, limited spring-driven Mechanic, and Advanced encounter with power jump and shield thrust. Current implementation evidence belongs in the [three-era review](three-era-movement-review.md) and [demonstration](../assets/audit/three-era-review/era-review.html). The [Stage Two review](stage-two-encounter-review.md) remains a historical checkpoint. Owner likeness and artistic acceptance remain pending; the prototype does not certify physical engineering or authorize publication.

## Phase gates

1. **Authority and guardrails:** keep the selected references, story distinctions, source paths, and all-rights-reserved status explicit. Unknown topology remains labeled as a proposal.
2. **Editable study:** review the full-body `.blend` against matched approved references; record owner likeness acceptance and any corrections before calling it accepted art.
3. **Local interaction slice:** inspect WebGL and illustrated fallback, orbit/zoom, part focus, open/explode/reassemble, era changes, deliberate reach and recovery, keyboard behavior, reduced motion, optional sound, and failure recovery. Record exactly which human checks and automated checks ran.
4. **Release review:** run `npm ci` and `npm run build`, inspect every `dist/` path and model URL, verify exact remote CI/deployment SHA and asset availability, and obtain explicit publication authorization. Local prototype presence or a successful build does not satisfy this gate.

No requirement in this record changes story canon, converts prototype geometry into engineering evidence, or treats a lead-selected raster or local scene as owner approval.
