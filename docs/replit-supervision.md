- After one nudge, verify acknowledgment and concrete progress. If two consecutive checkpoints produce no artifact and no live process, narrow or reassign the task; do not keep paying for repetitive status reports.
- Quota limits: checkpoint and stop. No paid fallback and no repeated retry loop; a quota retry opportunity must be at least six hours apart.
- Keep a private local state record of assignment, last evidence, last message time and disposition. Notify the owner only for a meaningful result, failure, required decision or changed blocker.
