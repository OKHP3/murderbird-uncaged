## Git LFS and provenance boundary

`git lfs ls-files --long` reports materialized (`*`) content for the selected candidate images, source v2 PNG masters, the Blender scale-stage file, legacy footage, and controlled pilot. Local files were hashed where listed above. This proves the local working tree has the binary payloads instead of text pointer stubs; it does not prove remote LFS availability, clean-clone retrieval, or deployment delivery. The imported source manifest at [overkill-hill-import-2026-09-26.json](../provenance/overkill-hill-import-2026-09-26.json) records source/destination paths, bytes, and hashes for imported materials. The v2 package manifest and consolidated register preserve older generation prompt, candidate, and derivative lineage; their presence is provenance evidence, not a global acceptance list.

The eight PNG narrative masters under `assets/murderbird/v2/masters/` and their generated derivatives are image candidates, not 3D source surfaces. The v2 package records the older four-era/seven-scene structure and the 1.8 m study. Preserve it as history. It is not the current three-era narrative contract, the selected 2.0 m reference package, or an animation-ready model.

## Capability gaps against the mandate

| Requirement | Current evidence | Gap before acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Recognizable exterior | One lead-accepted modern raster, separate era illustrations, and a post-baseline local editable study/export. | Matched-angle review and owner likeness acceptance remain open; current study surfaces and proportions are still an interpretation. |
| Real orbit and zoom | A post-baseline GLB now exists in the worktree; the earlier previs also has cameras. | Three.js loading and in-app orbit/zoom against coherent unseen-side geometry remain unverified. |
| Cage and grounded interaction | Selected stills ground the feet; the opening still includes a support. No selected image has cage bars. | Cage design, readable separation from the bird, collision surface, reach boundary, constrained contact animation and owner review. |
| Snap/claw and recovery | Historical attack/warning pictures and pose prompts exist; no accepted coherent cage-contact clip or 3D rig. | Articulated head/limb states, bounded reaction model, contact/recovery, cooldown, interruption handling, and keyboard/touch equivalents. |
| Opening and exploded view | Heart preview depicts an open chest in one still; the new study script defines separate shell and system groups. | Visual/structural review of the exported artifact, runtime controls, reversible separation and reassembly, and era-specific behavior remain to be demonstrated. |
| Earlier versus later systems | Story distinguishes repeated external/mechanical sequences from 2025 energy and learning. | Production geometry and explanation must keep finite energy separate from cognition, omit later subsystems in prior eras, and label unknown ancient internals as uncertain. |
| Audio | Accepted controlled clip is silent. Older MP4 AAC is present but unreviewed. Audio production elsewhere in the repo is separate music work. | No reviewed cage contact, mechanism, ambience, accessible controls, or story-safe exhibit mix is established. |

The minimum useful next asset is not another scene illustration: it is a reviewable, editable exterior blockout derived from candidate 03 and the selected head reference, with a separately proposed cage, a marked left-side repair area, and explicit uncertainty for unseen internals. Keep that prototype honestly labeled until the owner accepts its likeness and the selected interaction is demonstrated. This audit does not choose an authoring tool or certify that the existing app can meet the requirement without further implementation review.
