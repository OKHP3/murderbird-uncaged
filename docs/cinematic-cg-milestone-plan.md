| Milestone | Deliverables and visual pass criteria | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Reference and baseline | Complete scoped reference pins; lock canon-matched hero cameras; eight-angle baseline turntable and two hero comparisons. Pass requires exact reference identity, reproducible camera framing and visible baseline discrepancies. | 15 minutes / 3,000 tokens |
| 2 — Creature silhouette | Eight-angle silhouette turntable and neutral hero comparisons. Terror-bird proportions and compact wings; no crow, hawk, pigeon or oversized-wing read. | 45 minutes / 12,000 tokens |
| 3 — Cinematic appearance | Exhibit-lit turntable, two hero shots, head/breast close-ups. Canon-directed metal, optic and armor layering; no ghostlike or plastic finish. | 40 minutes / 10,000 tokens |
| 4 — Iron Verdict walk-on | Idle, looping walk and composed walk-on with final hero stills. Same creature identity and weight; folded wings; no distracting sliding, popping or flicker. | 45 minutes / 12,000 tokens |
