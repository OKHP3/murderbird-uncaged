## Matched-view visual assessment

I directly viewed candidate 03, the owner-preferred July head, and the latest matched side, head, front and three-quarter study captures. The captures show a clearer articulated neck gap and a more legible layered wing/body construction than the earlier smooth study. These are qualitative matched-view observations only: the reference illustrations use perspective, lighting and occlusion that differ from the neutral model cameras, so pixel-box ratios would imply precision the images cannot support. No exact height, width, joint coordinate, or production tolerance is inferred.

The current proposal still differs in identity cues. Its skull reads as a flat-topped, broad mechanical hood; the selected references have a swept crown and a deeper cheek/mandible contour surrounding the optic. The model’s bill is long and strongly hooked, but its pale faceted surfaces form a thin segmented arc instead of the references’ deep, continuous convex upper-bill profile. The neck separation is readable, yet the stacked straight plates look taller and more column-like than the selected compact curved bridge. The torso’s many repeated rows make it read as a uniform armored barrel; candidate 03’s breast is deep but the lower contour tapers toward the hips. The floor-planted bird feet remain useful, although the exposed shins still read somewhat long in the side capture. The leg geometry itself was retained in this pass; any change in its apparent exposed length comes from the revised body envelope and pose. The folded wing and compact rear stay within the selected silhouette; unseen far-side construction remains proposal.

Priority for another likeness pass: (1) reshape the skull crown, cheek and jaw mass together around the optic; (2) thicken and curve the bill profile while preserving its hook; (3) shorten and broaden the neck bridge without closing the articulation gap; (4) taper the lower breast into a defined pelvis; (5) review shin-to-body balance at matched camera scale. This is a candid discrepancy list, not an artistic approval or a claim that the model is recognizable or has met the owner’s exit condition.

## Direct source inspection and remaining limits

The current editable source is `assets/models/uncaged-structure-v1/murderbird-structure-v1.blend`; SHA-256 and the direct Blender result are in the receipt. Blender 5.2.1 opened the native source read-only and reported 1,276 editable mesh objects, 39 non-mesh objects, 63,468 evaluated triangles, and no armature. The scene hierarchy includes `murderbird → body → neck → head → jaw`, `body → left/right-mantle → left/right-wing-shield`, and independent rigid leg chains `murderbird → thigh → shin → foot → toes`. Six materials are assigned to those meshes: five neutral structural categories plus the Advanced amber sensor. Seven older material datablocks remain unused; their presence in the file does not mean those finishes are assigned. The validated GLB contains 23 meshes, 62 scene nodes, 63,468 triangles, zero skins, and one single-track head proof clip. This verifies a rigid editable proposal, not a finished skinned character or accepted material treatment.

The First Choice source bytes are technically verified and directly sampled at 0.4, 4 and 7 seconds. All three frames show the same workshop framing, separate support stand and planted feet; any body/head deformation at those samples is too subtle to distinguish confidently. The clip record describes a slight saddle rotation, restrained torso shift and eight-degree head lowering, and the owner accepted it for that story-page use only. It does not prove a three-dimensional joint or support design.

These captures should be reviewed at normal scale beside the selected July head and candidate 03 before any likeness gate is closed. The owner’s explicit likeness decision remains pending. No visual evidence here establishes that an accepted or recognizable final model has been achieved.
