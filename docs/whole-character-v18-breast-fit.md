# V18 breast and lower-neck fit study

This is a held regional construction candidate based on V17 attempt02. It is not selected by the application and has no artistic acceptance.

The latest candidate is [V18 attempt breast-fit08](../assets/models/whole-character-v18/attempt-breast-fit08/murderbird-whole-character-v18.blend), SHA-256 `a4f9ffd68067e14d5ff9e6abf8fc1d1c116d55692381d18673bfd4db5495147c`. Its composition receipt preserves the 52 pivots and parent graph, 462 authoring curves, materials, and all unmodified meshes. Two body-owned access ribs and three neck-owned throat laminas changed; the nominated flank laminas remained inside the new radial profile and were unchanged. The breast shell and all 78 breast plates remain intact.

The rib refit moves complete 12-vertex rings along the tube, preserving the section shape and retaining three rail-contact rings on each rib. Across the 21 supplied runtime-module samples, strict crossings in the scoped rib pairs fell from 132 on V17 to 12 on this candidate. All 12 candidate crossings occur during breast opening at separation zero: both ribs intersect the moving access shell at each of four open samples, and one first-course plate intersects rib `.003`. The breastplate pivot lies inside the shell planform, and its current vertical-axis swing carries the shell rearward into the body-fixed frame. The fit is therefore **REST-FIT ONLY / INSPECTION FAIL**. The next solution should correct the hinge path with a finite outer-boundary hinge or a supported translating hinge, then capture fresh poses; it should not keep moving the ribs to hide this conflict.

The lower-neck proposal uses a pivot-centered cylindrical contour (0.272 m central radius, 0.290 m outboard radius) on throat laminas 4–6. These dimensions are a reconstruction choice. The changed geometry leaves a dark slit and pointed hanging returns at the breast junction. Strict checks record 231 first-course/neck-guard pair-sample crossings across the 21 states, up from 85 on V17. The neck module is therefore held and must not be integrated.

The required next design is a coordinated breast opening lip and finite lower throat guard around the existing neck pivot. The access shell and first breast course belong to the breastplate assembly and move on inspection; lower guards remain rigidly attached to the neck. Their intended overlap and clearance must be designed together, preserving both owner transforms. Further surface clamps are not supported by this result.
