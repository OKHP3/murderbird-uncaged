# V36 compact-foot attempt03 — rejected, adjustment loop ended

The one permitted fit revision after the [Form01 rejection](whole-character-v36-checkpoint.md) did not produce an acceptable foot assembly. Removing the 22 mm arch raise and adding axial guard relief clears the targeted shifted-hinge and arch/shin pairs, but creates other guard/rail and hallux/truss crossings. It remains a regional failure, not an integrated improvement.

The [same bounded strict screen](../assets/audit/whole-character-v36/regional-studies/compact-feet/attempt03/attempt03-strict-foot-motion-screen.json) reports eight introduced pair identities in each of rest, claw scrape and landing, compared with the already-failed Form01 baseline. Four are between moving owners and four are within one owner. The screen was run by the worker who made the repair; it is not an additional independent approval. Its result is sufficient to reject this trial. No further adjustment iteration was authorized.

The first regional attempt03 native uses the V35 body; applying Form01's full pose packet failed the rest-parity guard because the bill contact markers differ. That failed setup is preserved. The subsequent evaluation derivative starts from the exact Form01 native and changes only the local foot guards/arch. It retains the Form01 node rests and bearing-local geometry. No screen thresholds or pair exclusions were loosened to obtain a pass. The preservation contract records 591 unrelated meshes and 171 bearing-local geometries unchanged; the 14 revised meshes are closed and finite, with two inherited tiny degenerate triangles retained on the left truss.

Evaluation native SHA-256: `289d1839801b7823d3a304ae89e48d34a3556f10a7d450c8d1fe9705aae2b79c`.

Original Form01 native and GLB are untouched. This regional trial has not been exported for the application, selected as default, pushed or deployed. The development default is still the unapproved V35 study; production remains V9. The three requested finished exterior states remain incomplete.

The next foot effort must reconcile the compact envelope, link paths, bearing seats, guards and hallux as one assembly. Repeated compression or isolated guard trimming is not producing coherent construction. The larger unresolved likeness priorities remain the head/optic profile, integrated shoulder mantle, breast/pelvis relationship and dense mechanical lower limbs. Do not use this repair's partial clash reduction as a reason to resume finishing materials or declare structural acceptance.
