This is an independent static-image critique, not owner acceptance, a new full score, or a motion/collision certificate.

The revised cheek and limb covers are concrete construction gains. The final refinement adds readable crown edge relief, more breast-side overlap and fuller lateral neck coverage. These changes improve the earlier candidate's construction, but the regular plate fields, sparse head enclosure and coarse limb/foot forms still prevent a completed likeness. F01–F04 remain open; the dependent surface pass remains inappropriate as a way to hide unresolved geometry.

The [frozen-pose receipt](../assets/audit/alignment-v3/frozen-extrema.json), generated `2026-09-28T03:12:04.863Z`, records both the local and served GLB as `c4dc308f77399410368b82443a1b21b9113cfedb258aa055906b4f13c92dda21`. All 20 frozen image files match their recorded bytes and SHA-256 values. I visually inspected 11 of those images: left/right jump, left/right thrust, left/right/head exhibit-light contact, and head/right Maker jaw at 0 and 100. I also inspected Advanced head/feet close-ups from the [browser receipt](../assets/audit/alignment-v3/browser-validation.json); both files match their recorded hashes. This is 13 inspected browser stills, not independent review of all 101 browser captures or any complete motion sequence.

- **Head and feet in the browser:** the regional construction seen in authoring views is present in the running derivative. Large cap gaps, a broad flat cheek arm, smooth long ankle guards, plain joint drums and bulbous claw roots remain visible. The browser lighting does not resolve these likeness findings.
- **Maker jaw:** the head and right-side pairs visibly show the lower jaw moving down as the control increases. The external control link follows the changed jaw position. This supports the corrected direction at the two displayed states. It does not establish every intermediate clearance, an anatomically complete closing fit, or the absence of deformation throughout a sweep.
- **Advanced jump:** both side views show the feet clearly above the floor and the legs folded beneath the body at the recorded airborne phase, approximately `0.422`. Their arrangement reads as a compact crouch. No landing sequence was reviewed here; these stills do not prove balance, support transfer, swept clearance or natural timing.
- **Advanced thrust:** at the recorded phase, approximately `0.491`, both feet appear planted and the torso is braced over flexed legs. The mantle remains close to the flank; a strong wing excursion is not readily communicated by this single bilateral pose. A full sequence is necessary to judge the intended thrust and its recovery. Exposed dark/brass actuation links are visible, but their attachment clearance cannot be certified from the images.
- **Advanced contact:** the exhibit-light views show the selected vertical rail beside the bill, correcting the earlier neutral-light evidence's missing target. The gold ring sits above the contact region and still visually suggests a target, although it is an attention reticle. The receipt reports the sampled bill surface point at approximately `[0, 1.2194, 2.0790]`, with zero neck/skull translation error. That is recorded geometric evidence, not force testing or an independent image-based measurement. The views support a forward braced contact posture; they do not prove the complete approach, release or interruption behavior.

These bounded observations add visible evidence for downward jaw articulation, an airborne pose, planted bracing and a shown rail target. They leave the likeness findings and the need for normal-speed acting, supported claw action, full phase/interruption coverage and comprehensive clearance review open. No motion acceptance or owner acceptance is supplied by this addendum.
